## Ownership

Mathematical chapters own arguments. The claim ledger owns proposition identity,
preconditions, dependencies, and status history. Mechanism pages explain how
current code produces, checks, or consumes that evidence. Original archives own
what was tried and measured; they do not select the next task.

Keep one current sequencing owner in HANDOFF. Do not copy a milestone's result
or next recommendation into every index. [Generated research views](solver/research-state.md) expose declared quantitative
inputs and unavailable evidence through the existing reporter.

Links are available references, not recursive reading obligations. Preserve
useful counterexamples when recommendations are disproved. Update the affected
canonical owner, keep original evidence accessible, and check edited links.
A small fix needs no new plan, claim, research packet, or archive.
