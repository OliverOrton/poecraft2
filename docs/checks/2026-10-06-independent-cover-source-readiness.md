## Mathematical construction and complete scope premises

Let the resolved goal have `g` disjoint native member slots and require `k` satisfied slots. Native layout construction rejects overlapping member masks. A slot may still contain alternative satisfying modifiers with different exclusion groups; the cover does not combine those blockers into a probability model. Let `M(x)` be every slot currently satisfied in the physical native member `x`.

For each retained, finite-price primitive `a` in the immutable resolved registry, take a support superset `R(a)` of every goal slot it could acquire under any admitted item-tag signature. Existing all-tag explicit-affix reach supplies this superset for known primitives. Companion-state, newer unclassified primitive roles and unknown synthetic roles receive every goal slot as an optimistic paid exit. The synthetic exact Restart has no acquisition support. All retained primitive roles are scanned, including dependencies that are not independent candidate actions. Adding such auxiliary purchases only weakens the lower; it does not change caller permissions.

The registry's materialized Fossil loadouts are the declared product envelope. Deferred signatures outside that beam are not silently claimed to be the unrestricted game's whole Fossil powerset. Current state-local generated operators compose this immutable retained primitive registry; they do not append new registry primitives. The known static and generated enum ranges are pinned. Unknown generated bits, unsupported authored kinds, missing runtime paths, restore-bearing authored programmes, or a fully priced authored wrapper with an unpriced runtime dependency refuse the selected cover. Future grammar changes therefore require extending this support proof rather than inheriting permission from a similarly named source.

Each proof purchase costs `c_down(a)`, no greater than either downward exact resource pricing or the actual native primitive row-price arithmetic. The source checks the native primitive prefix against the registry index and uses its native resource-vector order. Missing/negative/nonfinite prices cannot provide a finite proof purchase. A malformed primitive prefix or native price overflow weakens a purchase to zero. Proof additions round down; native probabilities, costs and resource quantities are unchanged. The preparation refuses an unsupported floating-point rounding mode.

Define `H(0)=0` and allow a purchase `a` to add any subset of `R(a)` to an ever-acquired mask, at cost `c_down(a)`. The monotone subset dynamic programme gives the cheapest optimistic purchase cost `H(S)`. Define

`C(M) = min { H(S) : popcount(M union S) >= k }`.

Every held goal receives perfect retention; rarity changes, clean-shape preparation, side/capacity constraints, tier blockers, removals and cleanup are granted freely. A dirty member with all required slots therefore has cover zero. This is intentional and prevents W2's incomplete terminal-debt charge. Retry/compressed-memory markers receive zero rather than being interpreted as physical members. Generated Imprint programmes disable this route, and static ImprintRetry programmes refuse it. Non-imprint option semantics reject checkpoint dependencies. The selected domain is neutral Current, noncluster, Rare, clean `ForbidUnmatched`, with no additional prefix/suffix count ranges. Any-k uses the native required-slot union conversion. Unsupported contexts retain zero from this component.

**Theorem, under the stated immutable law/goal/price/grammar premises:** `C(M(x))` is a lower bound on the expected remaining paid cost of every proper native policy in the declared scope. On each successful physical execution, credit initially held goals permanently and every newly acquired goal through a purchase whose support includes that primitive's possible acquisition. Credit removals, conditional choices and all structural work optimistically. The resulting cover path has cost no greater than the execution's actual paid primitive cost. A successful terminal member has at least `k` credited slots. Thus each successful path costs at least `C(M(x))`; properness and nonnegative costs justify taking expectation, including all positive-probability paid failure/retry branches. Infinite expected cost does not invalidate this lower. No draw probability, representative member, survival estimate, target-only denominator or parent-graph optimum appears in the construction.

The theorem covers adaptive controller composition only because every paid primitive in every legal production is covered, restore is excluded, and the price/primitive laws remain fixed through the invocation. It does not permit taking a restricted graph optimum as a global lower, importing a certificate from a different goal/economy/registry generation, or restoring hidden saved state through a physical mask. The new source certificate is invocation-local, stores the exact source member, and has no portable/cache import path. Publication checks its exact source and consumption gate. Prices are copied into SolveWork; repricing creates a new invocation and a new cover. A future reusable certificate would require the full immutable context identity, not just matching the source item.

For primitive or admitted automatic action `o`, the selected OperatorLower grants every held goal and the union of all constituent acquisition support for free, then adds its native immediate cost. An automatic option's immediate cost is retained only after its exact state-local OptionKernel has published the complete expected resource vector, including paid failures/retries. For an authored fixed option, use the minimum paid first primitive across **all** native runtime execution paths, rather than the first listed preparation. A prepared Fracture carrier may execute the conditional Fracture without Chaos preparation. An empty path, if supplied, grants zero. The authored floor is not a complete immediate-program authority and is excluded from descriptor retirement. Exact Restart adds only the fresh universal continuation `C(0)`; shaped clean/carrier maxima cannot enter.

These action floors never call the nested carrier/debt maximum on neutral scope. For an actual outcome, the optimistic granted union contains its held and newly acquired slots, so its cover continuation cannot exceed the actual remaining cost; adding an admissible immediate charge preserves the action lower. OrdinaryClean's old branch remains a separate owner and receives no new proof endorsement here.
