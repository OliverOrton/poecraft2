## Objective

Determine exactly why the full primitive solver envelope becomes trapped in
synchronous setup when Fossil is admitted, retain a narrow behavior-neutral
repair, and qualify the existing carrier ladder under the genuinely full
primitive envelope.

The work must improve or cooperatively bound the measured setup owner without
changing which Fossil actions exist, how they are priced, how they transition,
or which actions the product admits. Once full-scope execution becomes
observable and cancellable, the boundary must establish whether the existing
ladder remains correctly generated, serviced, assembled, compiled, and
independently evaluated with Fossil present.

This is not a fragment boundary and not permission to repair an inferred
ladder defect before full-scope evidence identifies one.
