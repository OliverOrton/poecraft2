## Four research questions

1. How can a generic bounded controller compose the proven partial-held s3
   continuation while preserving exact blockers, tiers, capacity, setup/cleanup,
   every positive entry and proper paid recovery? Is the historical word admissible
   in the original priced product envelope?
2. Where does supported capability cease to become a retained Current policy:
   grammar, proposal/orientation enumeration, evaluation/admission, scheduling or
   incumbent retention? Identify a falsifiable source omission. Forced proposal3
   evidence is not normal enumeration; bounded grammar is not all-search coverage.
3. Which state-dependent reroll/plain Exalt/blocked Exalt/EExalt choices explain
   the historical executable cost advantage under fully matched semantics? Quantify
   reach/visit-weighted effects and full-root properness, preserving failed plainfill
   evidence. Legal local words and the unqualified fixed-stage210k candidate are
   insufficient economic evidence.
4. Can Current/Finder/authored consumer budget, lifecycle and scheduling authority
   be proved separately? Explain product bounded finish separately from private
   stops, including ordinary+automatic debit, query/full identity, incumbent, census,
   rollback/cancel and qualified aggregate measurements.
