## Authority Distinction

- An arbitrary-entry continuation certificate proves only a proper, finite,
  exactly evaluated fixed-policy upper from one exact item.
- A candidate potential entry has that `J_policy` value but no lower authority.
- A certified entry belongs to an identity-compatible dependency SCC whose
  selected kernel and every caller-authorized action constraint close
  simultaneously against certified peers or the existing admissible `L0`.
- The greatest certified shadow subset remains observational. Ordinary public
  lower and lifecycle authority are unchanged.

Every post-deviation item must enter the compiled strategy through its global
router. Canonical physical item identity and full equality checks are required;
digests and local node/state numbers are lookup aids only.
