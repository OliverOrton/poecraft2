## Primary research used for the recommendation

- **P1:** Alur et al., *Syntax-Guided Synthesis*, FMCAD 2013. [Publisher abstract](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/6679385/); [author page](https://www-cad.eecs.berkeley.edu/~sseshia/pubs/b2hd-alur-fmcad13.html). Relevance: candidate grammar and semantic specification are separate; a search/ranker cannot express a program absent from its grammar. No new theorem or syntax-guided solver is proposed.
- **P2:** Sutton, Precup and Singh, *Between MDPs and semi-MDPs: A framework for temporal abstraction in reinforcement learning*, 1999. [Publisher](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0004370299000521). Relevance: options have control/termination structure rather than being arbitrary unguarded primitive lists. Native paid cleanup and legal initiation still require their own engine contract.
- **P3:** Andriushchenko et al., *PAYNT: A Tool for Inductive Synthesis of Probabilistic Programs*, CAV 2021. [Publisher](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-81685-8_40). Relevance: sketch/realization/checker separation. No import of exhaustive finite-family guarantees, model checker or claimed speedup into this bounded SSP finder.

Only primary research supports the technical literature discussion. No external source supplies game mechanics, measured poecraft2 performance, or an expected improvement for the proposed implementation.
