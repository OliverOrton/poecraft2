# Selected D0–D4 milestones

## D0 — Scope, existing evidence and workload localization

Read current main/HANDOFF and the completed G0 record. Verify the existing raw
report identities in [DATA_REQUEST.md](DATA_REQUEST.md), then extract the current
aggregate timing, work, memory and candidate phases from those saved reports.
Do not reinterpret final post-release memory as construction peak or subtract
nested timers from wall time without establishing their domains.

Use the actual six-case corpus. A4 is the primary because it has saved full-run
and G0 evidence and genuinely heterogeneous prefix members. Preserve its ordinary
240-second Finish, native 300/host315 deadlines, 1-GiB aggregate, 50M parent
logical-work and other inherited caps. Restart and generated Imprint remain off;
native retention activation is `reuse`. A 35-second diagnostic is a different
clock profile and cannot establish full-run final quality.

If compatible full-duration *served-work* evidence is absent, perform one bounded
instrumented full-duration A4 run, through the existing runner. It must cover
pre-first-policy, post-first-policy and finish/checking rather than replay the
previous first-row slice. If the first useful witness appears earlier, stop the
additional diagnostic at that decision; retain its actual limited scope. Do not
blindly run the whole six-case cohort.

D0 exits with a timing/denominator map and whether the proposed later-stage
subsystems account for potentially material cost. Missing phase attribution is
unknown; the entire unaccounted remainder is not assigned to Bellman work.

## D1 — Work-weighted role recurrence, with bounded native evidence

Use [DIAGNOSTIC_AND_DESIGN.md](DIAGNOSTIC_AND_DESIGN.md). Collect lightweight
role descriptors and actual task completions at existing owner boundaries,
with independent binding/state/domain identity. Obtain exact counters where
cheap and bounded stratified examples elsewhere. No transition-by-transition JSON.

Report descriptive families, operationally compatible families and actual
reusable-work candidates separately. Distinguish repeated attempts of one binding
from different bindings. Preserve legacy scheduling-class identity as a comparison,
not the only candidate grouping. Do not use its hashes as an exact reuse guard.

Initially follow A4 attained-prefix states, suffix context and failure/recovery
states. Include B3/B4 or A3 only for a declared missing contrast or second-binding
witness. Do not force 24 first requests to stand for the whole downstream workload.

For the strongest *at most three* families, estimate an optimistic bound on work
that could be removed, then identify unavoidable numeric/output/validation work.
Inspect one bounded closed-with-ports neighborhood per family, deepening beyond
one transition only where the proposed structure actually spans several decisions.
Recommended investigative ceiling: 3 regions, each at most 64 inspected local
nodes and 256 inspected rows, with every additional outcome retained as an explicit
port/unknown obligation. This limit is for diagnostic inspection only; it never
truncates native laws or creates an accepted controller.

D1 gate: a real heterogeneous pair with an expensive identified repeated operation,
a native pre-work guard, actual repeat frequency and credible finite all-in benefit.
A million histogram matches or all computed signatures matching is not sufficient.
As a proposed priority screen, require plausible removable work of at least 5% of
the measured full-run active work, or a concrete original-cap capacity unlock.
Unknown/nonexclusive cost attribution does not pass this screen. A smaller
critical-path saving needs an identified useful ready-candidate/deadline chain,
not speculation that every microsecond changes final quality.
If the first family fails for a concrete reason, inspect the next ranked family;
do not automatically declare all downstream structures equivalent or impossible.
Stop after the three cost-ranked candidates unless a new owner decision selects
another measured premise.

## D2 — One downstream computation-reuse pilot

Select ONE mechanism after D1. Preferred: a native-owned continuation build plan
whose menu, dependencies, internal graph/ordering and exit ports are reused over
actual different role bindings. Secondary: a measured sparse selection/evaluation
structure or common subexpression that safely avoids repeated structural work.

The fixed structural part is immutable. Numerical coefficients, scratch, current
value inputs and native output mappings belong to each binding. If the region has
cycles, use the existing solver/checker for each bound system; a shared symbolic
pattern does not license reusing a numeric factorization. Missing external
continuations stay typed unknowns until compatible evidence exists.

Feed the resulting complete rows or checked candidate back to the existing owner.
The source path must show a second binding avoiding the selected structural work.
Building it fully first and deduplicating afterward qualifies only as measured
storage sharing, not construction saving.

Cold fallback is required for failed/unsupported guards. No changed candidate
vocabulary, ranking, pruning, rollout horizon, prices, goal semantics or scope in
the reuse comparison. A separate scheduling treatment requires a new decision.

## D3 — Proportional qualification

First replay the *actual finite binding workload*, ordinary existing caches on,
with/without the one treatment. Include first build, matching, parameter binding,
numeric passes, mapping, output and evidence checks. Use one counterbalanced
repeat for a material timing result. Artificially multiplying calls or disabling
baseline caches cannot support the headline.

Proposed reuse threshold: >=25% reduction in the declared repeated structural work
AND >=10% improvement in the whole relevant workload, or identical complete outputs
inside an originally binding capacity. Also report its share of the whole run and
first-policy/finalization effect. Thresholds are proposed gates, not predictions.
An expensive region can be relevant even without a new policy; a micropercentage
of a cheap region does not establish a large solver gain.

After the native witness, qualify affected A4/A3 and one bow context, reusing
compatible controls. A5/B5 remain suffix extensions selected when affected or
needed for the complete user-facing scope. Do not rerun all six at every patch.

Separate economic success: >=20% cheaper original-price, independently checked
full-root controller against the fresh compatible baseline, without replacing a
stronger retained compatible incumbent. A3/B3 partial items and a 35-second run
are not roots/profiles for that percentage. A new feasible policy is its own outcome.

Preserve the recovered ordinary C4 result, C5 first-policy delivery, exact Regalia
and the bounded ordinary Ring-two reference where shared owners are touched.
Wide Bow/Ring/Amulet measurements are different profiles. Keep accepted historical
C4 2.643-second ordinary-call tradeoff visible, no false 250-ms pass. Qualify new
material latency/cancel/Finish regressions instead of silently permitting them.

## D4 — Knowledge integration, disposition, stop

Integrate [MATHEMATICS.md](MATHEMATICS.md) into canonical representation and
policy/search owners with actual premises, then mechanism changes only if retained.
Keep G0, pair-stop/memo and memory negatives at their original scopes. Update the
relevant existing claim histories/RQ-003 without asserting novelty from old algebra
or native correctness from synthetic fixtures.

Report one of: useful guarded reuse; useful but limited throughput/storage result;
insufficient recurrence; recurrence with dominant unavoidable numeric/output cost;
missing native structural contract; or unresolved evidence. No broad “symmetry
failed” or “symmetry is proved big” from this finite programme.

Stop after two substantive failed pilot variants without a new causal premise.
Remove unused runtime scaffolding after a negative, retain small reproducible
observations/arguments. Do not revive the removed memory patches, stopping-set
variants or a new broad solver rewrite. No push or automatic successor.
