# Native diagnostic and implementation design

## 1. What is being measured

The unit is a *native work item*, not a photograph of a state and not expected
future crafting visits. Repeated invocation of the same state/action must be
separated from two different native bindings of a relative structure.

Use existing owner events and counters. Candidate observations can join
`AutomaticCandidateRecord`, row completion, action-envelope service, numerical
pass generations, assertion and portfolio events. Do not create a parallel truth
store or mutate scheduling phases for the diagnostic. A missing event remains
missing; a final-state record does not reconstruct its creation-time cost.

The instrument may be a private opt-in benchmark sink or a small existing telemetry
extension. Keep it out of normal hot paths when disabled, charge any retained native
storage, and expose capacity/omission counts. Use a native elapsed/active-resume
clock for work spans; coroutine lifetime and nested child work are separate.

## 2. Keep the populations distinct

A compact row/state census needs these fields where available:

- invocation and calculator/layout generation; native source and operator identity;
- first discovered and queue/service observation, exact terminal/retry-basin role;
- owner lane: ordinary outer expansion, automatic child construction, focused
  numerical work, strict refinement, private candidate, compiler or evaluator;
- complete/pending/inapplicable/refused/capped outcome and current generation;
- exact-cache hit, exact-payload sharing, existing option-template reuse and cached
  expected-value service when those are the relevant owner;
- actual attempted/completed rows, support entries and coefficient/choice visits;
- exclusive build/structural/numeric/output work where separable; inclusive child
  timers explicitly marked, never added as independent time;
- retained bytes and concurrent owner/scratch overlap, not a sum of unrelated peaks;
- descriptive role family, native binding ID and costed operational family.

Initial provenance may identify the broad-reforge row that first interned a state.
Do not store all ancestral paths. A state can have multiple producers; record
first-source plus ambiguity/other-source counts and never claim causal cost
attribution to one predecessor merely because it was observed first.

Expansion-start flag/count, complete action coverage and expanded private/evaluator
states are different counters. Use actual activation flags (`focused_mode`,
`incremental_action_generation`, producer domain), not a phase label guessed from
where the run ended. [R6–R12]

## 3. Two signatures and a stronger operational witness

### H0: descriptive role signature (diagnostic only)

Canonicalize the requested three-prefix roles under at most their six permutations.
Retain per-role missing/below/satisfied status, crafted/fractured state and side;
retain overall rarity, exact goal/clean classification, capacity, current suffix
requirements/status, native protection/influence/Eldritch/offer/checkpoint context.
Record the actual physical-to-role map separately. Role permutation is a matching
proposal, not a promise that an action with a corresponding native meaning exists.

Do not omit blockers or four categories of junk from the record. A cheap aggregate
may be used for H0 grouping if its exact source records remain available for H1;
that omission makes it *coarser diagnostic similarity*, never reuse authority.
Do not use weights to exclude all heterogeneous comparisons. Numeric vectors,
prices and member leaves are binding inputs. Changes that alter support/legality
or observation dependencies must still be represented or cause guard refusal.

### H1: operational signature for one declared computation

Refine an H0 family using the actual work's required predicates and action schema:
legal menu and role-to-native action map; side/capacity and all relevant conflicts;
ordered setup/attempt/cleanup and information timing; selected members/required
levels; joint outcomes; local graph or dependency pattern; actual exit-port mapping.

Use current backward observation contracts to justify which facts matter. Unknown
requirements are not “don't care”. Missing an action is distinct from failing its
price, state legality, continuation coverage or scheduling service.

Literal background may be held fixed initially. Removing its labels later needs a
native relation. Do not canonicalize away a suffix that changes a targeting pool,
a lock, an offered option or an outside continuation.

### H2: guarded work-region witness

For a chosen pair, exhibit (1) the code operation to avoid, (2) its complete input
and output dependencies, (3) a structure obtainable before repeating it, and
(4) a legal binding specialization producing the same native result as cold work.

The same integer row degree or same abstract menu is not a graph isomorphism.
Nor does matching a constructed graph yield a cheap guard automatically. Record
which matching operations require full native support construction; that cost
belongs on the reused path if production would still require it.

Only H2 can support implementation. Even H2 does not transfer lower/upper authority
without the corresponding bound computation and existing native acceptance.

## 4. Work weighting and sampling

The first output is a table per owner/phase/H0/H1 family:

```text
observed unique bindings | started/completed work | exact-cache hits
pending/refused count | first/last observation | structural operations
numeric visits | output entries | exclusive active ns | inclusive child ns
candidate structures | selected/checked participation | retained/scratch bytes
```

If per-item timing would disturb tight loops, aggregate visits by existing batch,
use bounded deterministic representative samples, and preserve inclusion/omission
coverage. Do not apportion a mixed batch's whole time to each member. Exact counts
are preferable where cheap; sample-only results are sample-only. Do not export
an unbounded census or run canonicalization inside every Bellman multiply-add.

Use early, post-first-verified and late/pre-Finish strata. Fixed windows and limits
must be recorded; the 35-second G0 run cannot represent the late 240-second regime.
Stratify high-goal-progress and failure/cleanup states rather than admitting only
states that happened to survive an already restrictive clean-entry selector.

Most useful: time actually paid beyond the first binding for a *guardable structural
operation*. Least useful: raw number of equal masks. A separately measured same-
state/generation rebuild may be a conventional optimization, but is not claimed as
heterogeneous cross-binding reuse.

Reuse of the current exact-kernel or value cache has to be subtracted before
estimating opportunity. For numeric-only rows, variable coefficients and successor
values usually still have to be read. Cheap symbolic setup cannot save the whole
numeric pass. A result dominated by this fact is an informative negative.

## 5. The preferred shared object

Use an immutable native-owner **continuation computation structure** with:
- finite internal operation/decision dependency pattern;
- typed binding leaves for actual probabilities, rewards and roles;
- support/legality/observation guards and authoritative generation keys;
- exact local-to-native and external-port maps;
- explicit output kind: row law, construction result, selection plan or candidate.

Binding work owns numeric arrays, selected decisions, currently supplied outside
values, physical IDs and scratch. Do not put old scalar values in the shared object.
A template may be shared while bound state IDs remain distinct.

For a fixed selected local controller:

    (I - Q_b) v_b = c_b + H_b u_b.

For a local decision problem, each admissible native action has its own row in the
corresponding Bellman expression. The action min is recomputed per binding under
the current numeric/properness contract. Cycles use the existing numerical owner,
not a new symbolic parameter-region optimizer.

Every omitted local successor is an explicit boundary port. `u_b` is either a real
compatible continuation input or stays unknown. A port that can return to the
region must be evaluated in the consistent composed controller; treating it as
an independent terminal tail is invalid. Hidden action/program choices remain at
their native observation point.

A local cut at 64 nodes in the diagnostic does not change the full controller. It
may make the proposed interface too large or too unknown to be useful. Report it;
never assign free recovery to force a small region.

## 6. Interaction with existing ownership

Keep the object where the avoided structure is owned: candidate/continuation builder,
sparse selection work or evaluator. Do not add unrelated permanent fields to
SolveWork::Impl or create another scheduler. Cold and specialized routes must
produce the existing output type at the same completed-evidence boundary.

Store shape, binding and numeric/evidence generation separately. A price change
may preserve a genuinely price-independent pattern but invalidates priced values
and price-sensitive admission decisions. Changed boundary data invalidates numeric
results even when local probabilities do not change. Active support, tier-member
observer changes and role remapping require their own guard checks.

Charge shared storage once and all simultaneous binding work/scratch. In-flight
eviction retains referenced storage until release. Cancel releases uncommitted
binding work without destroying the verified portfolio or refunding logical work.
Partial rows/regions are not normal cache entries. Existing exact equality and
numerical tolerances are never weakened to a role hash.

## 7. Admissible outcomes

- Strong: a structure really reused for unequal native bindings, with cold-agreeing
  laws/decisions and measured all-in savings consumed by the solver.
- Narrow but useful: construction memory or a completed workload improves without
  a new cheapest policy. Report it as such.
- Negative: descriptive recurrence collapses under relevant context; guard costs
  as much as rebuilding; numeric/output work dominates; frequency insufficient;
  or all candidate sharing is already captured by current caches.

An alternate role-based *search ordering* might still be worth future research,
but it changes the workload rather than sharing it. It is not silently added to
this pilot if the structure-reuse gate fails.
