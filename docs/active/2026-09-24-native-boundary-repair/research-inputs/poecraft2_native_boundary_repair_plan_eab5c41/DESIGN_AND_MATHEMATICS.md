# Native-context repair: contracts and derivations

The following are conditional mathematical specifications and exact toy examples.
They are not new native certificates, a mechanics implementation, or evidence that
the proposed family is economically successful. Existing native numerical acceptance
and full original-root evaluation remain authoritative. [R8,R10,R11]

## 1. Separate three predicates

For a physical item/control entry x and candidate programme m, distinguish:

1. **Boundary safety S(x):** compiler-authored genuine decision, correct immutable
   graph/request identity, complete observable entry, no unaccounted hidden
   checkpoint/offer, and an actual valid global route where donor continuation is used.
2. **Candidate initiation I_m(x):** native legality and complete refinement/program
   contracts in that context, plus the programme-specific setup/exit requirements.
3. **Search selection H_m(x):** a deliberately bounded ordering/coverage choice.

The old clean family used a narrow I/H combination: ordinary persistent context,
Rare, goal-only occupied affixes and exactly one missing goal. Failure there does
not imply failure of S or I for every other m. Conversely, S alone cannot establish
an arbitrary candidate's legality or properness. [R5,R6]

A count excluded by the first-primitive-name filter is not a native action-refusal
count. A boolean that combines rarity, persistent context and goal debt cannot
identify their separate frequencies. Source guards and native measurements must be
reported at their actual resolution.

## 2. The semantic action is a complete programme

Let a genuine decision state be z=(item,control). A native macro m runs its
prescribed internal operations and observable choices until its next allowed
boundary or goal. Assume its internal execution terminates almost surely with
finite expected cost, and that its complete exit law is represented.

Write c_m(z) for its entire expected paid cost and P_m(z,z') for its boundary
transition law. The goal is an explicit zero-tail exit. A completed macro may use
many primitive actions; treating it as one decision step does not reduce its price
or primitive reward. Partial/unsupported internal cycles are not normalized away.

This is the standard option/SMDP distinction [P1], instantiated here through the
existing evaluator's selected-program contract [R8]. That contract describes the
OLD selected macro only. A new alternative requires its own native law. Reusing
old transition probabilities because the first emitted action looks similar is
invalid.

A fixed native controller can be flattened into its operation/item graph; the
independent full check remains the acceptance route. We are not introducing a new
macro correctness oracle or claiming every existing selected kernel is available
without additional evaluator requests.

## 3. Bounded repair followed by an actual base policy

Let D be the domain on which the immutable base policy pi has a compatible finite
proper continuation V_pi. Define W_0(z)=V_pi(z) for z in D, W_0(goal)=0, and leave
other inputs unavailable (not economically zero or a proved infinite optimum).

For a finite declared candidate set M_h(z), define:

  W_h(z) = min { V_pi(z), c_m(z) + sum_{z'} P_m(z,z') W_{h-1}(z') }

where V_pi is an option only when available and a macro term is admitted only
when ALL positive-probability exits have supported finite W_{h-1} values. Zero-
probability exits need not be queried; a tiny positive probability may not be dropped.

The index h counts COMPLETE planner decisions. It is neither a simulation limit
nor an expected-action penalty nor the user's hard primitive budget.

### Conditional feasible-prefix theorem

Assume the represented finite local domain, every admitted macro's complete native
law and finite expected duration/cost, compatible proper base tails, and that the
selected decisions can be compiled using their actual permitted observations.
Then finite induction constructs a proper executable policy for every finite
W_h: execute the chosen macro, decrement the staged control counter at its genuine
end, or enter an immutable base-policy mode. With finitely many stages, finite
expected local reward plus a finite proper base continuation gives finite expected
root cost and almost-sure goal completion. On D, W_h <= V_pi because immediate
base handoff is an available candidate.

This is a restricted feasible-policy result. It does not assert optimality over
all native policies, a lower bound on V*, or a useful computational complexity.
It is related to rollout/multistep improvement [P2], but does not depend on
approximate aggregation.

### Missing tails and nonuniform observations

If one tail is outside D, the complete candidate may be refused or repaired by
another allowed local step. Existing arbitrary-entry native certification may
establish that the old router is valid there. It must actually do so; a matching
coarse mask is not a proof. [R12]

Two physically different members with the same emitted predicate may require
different decisions. A per-exact-item calculation does not authorize an
unobservable branch. Either a native whole-member contract justifies one decision,
the existing compiler/refinement observes the distinguishing fact, or the proposal
is refused. No representative-only law or hidden offered-choice selection.

## 4. Why two complementary decisions can matter

Toy: the base costs 10 at x and 100 at y. From x, pay 1 to prepare y. From y,
a complete native-like alternative pays 1 to finish. A one-step repair at x sees
1+100 and keeps the base. A two-stage repair pays 2. Improving y while never making
it reachable from x changes the original root by zero.

This motivates testing a bounded setup-and-follow-through bundle. It does not
claim that a particular PoE blocker/removal gives that gain. Every actual lost-goal
branch, side constraint and cleanup is retained.

## 5. Staged and recurring controllers are not interchangeable

The finite-prefix argument needs real control state h and a permanent base mode.
The same item reached in two stages may have different prescribed decisions. A
cache keyed only by physical item can silently erase the stage and change the
controller.

The current general composer may intentionally reenter a local region through the
global router. That is a stationary recurring replacement, not the finite prefix.
Use it only with its stated semantics and complete whole-root evaluation. Do not
advertise W_h as its value or infer properness from the finite-depth search tree.
If the generated local choices conflict by stage, compile the stages faithfully
through existing graph namespaces or refuse that interpretation; never collapse
silently. [R10,R11]

Toy: base cost1/success0.1 has value10. A replacement cost2/success0.5 costs7 for
one attempt then the base, but4 when used on every revisit. Both can be valid,
with different laws. Two locally terminating fragments can also cycle forever
when connected; local termination alone does not prove a recurring replacement.

The design intentionally does not require one-shot ranking to predict recurring
ranking. The complete candidate graph and original-root cost are the actual result.

## 6. Exact root consequences and selection

A local item is not a free initial condition. All full-root graphs retain the
original paid prefix and native goal. A 50-cost acquisition followed by an improved
cost4 tail costs54, not4. Complete original-root checking determines whether the
candidate improves the actual donor/current best.

For a proper fixed controller, expected immediate spend can be decomposed as
J(root)=sum_z d_pi(z)c_pi(z), on a disjoint complete decision partition. A selected
macro includes its internal cost; do not also add the same internal primitive
spend. Visits times remaining value is generally overlapping exposure, not spend
or recoverable gain. [R11]

For a common finite domain and proper old/new controllers, the performance-
difference identity uses the NEW controller's occupancy. A repeated local return
can greatly amplify or alter a one-use estimate. This programme need not build a
new response matrix or occupancy-ranking engine to exploit a witnessed difference.
Full native reevaluation remains mandatory for changed emitted controllers.

## 7. Heterogeneous roles without false symmetry

A candidate family can resolve preserved-role set, missing-role set, side capacity
and context into different native choices for each instance. Its weights, tiers,
blockers, prices and observed states remain actual binding inputs. Two inputs may
share construction code but require different output laws and chosen policies.

The important extension now is a useful INITIATION/CONSTRUCTION domain at real
controller boundaries, not a demonstrated cross-binding speedup. A new wrapper
around the same calls is not evidence that expensive work was reused. Do not claim
an exact role quotient or a general compiler template result from this programme.

## 8. Resource and authority induction

At each cooperative boundary, committed donor evidence remains intact; incomplete
local kernels/graphs stay private. Charge parent, donor/provenance, child, new graph,
checker, staged values and overlapping frames simultaneously. Release changes live
storage, not cumulative native work already spent. Cached values bind full target,
entry/control, graph, prices, layout, support and relevant generations.

An unavailable comparison is not a refusal certificate for the global action
universe. Full-scope lower/exact closure cannot use a restricted local value as a
lower. A newer verified incumbent can supersede the frozen donor during service;
publication compares with that current compatible winner and seals its matching
graph/certificate/context/value. [R11,R17]

## 9. Research and documentation status

Initiation sets and temporally extended actions are classical [P1]. Base-policy
lookahead is classical [P2]. The engineering/research contribution to test is a
resource-bounded native correspondence from genuine compiler boundaries through
actual persistent/dirty contexts to complete checked recurring or staged policies.
No originality, runtime improvement or native theorem is claimed from these
algebraic examples alone.
