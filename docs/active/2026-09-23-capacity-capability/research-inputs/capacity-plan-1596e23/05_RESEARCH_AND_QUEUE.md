# Research assessment and the next larger directions

The papers below inform the design; repository measurements decide which code to change. The literature review is targeted, not an exhaustive novelty determination. No external PoE mechanics were researched.

## 1. Action generation versus full successor construction

Schmalz and Trevizan's CG-iLAO* work views heuristic SSP search through constraint generation, postponing unpromising actions until needed. The author's current page identifies the AIJ 2026 paper as the extended version superseding earlier versions and fixing minor issues. Its broad relevance is avoiding action computations that do not yet need service. [P1]

That does not authorize treating a resource-refused poecraft2 kernel as complete or absent. Poecraft2 already has incremental action generation, lower-guided deferral and complete native program kernels. The selected question is the much narrower one exposed by the cap witness: can one optional failed construction remain an unresolved obligation while another useful complete policy is built? Deferral for resource allocation is not the paper's admissible economic pruning theorem.

## 2. Partial search with upper witnesses

Bounded RTDP (McMahan, Likhachev and Gordon, 2005) maintains upper/lower bounds and can solve start-focused tasks without touching the whole state space. That is useful precedent for keeping a feasible controller while selectively expanding. [P2]

Here, an executable upper is not merely a heuristic value table. Every positive-mass native exit must have a compatible continuation and the emitted graph must pass the existing evaluator. The current retained portfolio, proper-policy machinery and private contexts are assets; do not build a second RTDP engine or assume that touching fewer states makes an incomplete policy executable.

## 3. Independent acceptance is particularly important for memory work

Fixed Point Certificates for Reachability and Expected Rewards in MDPs (TACAS 2025) develops finite-MDP certificates and a formally verified checker. It supports separating a proposal/optimization algorithm from its acceptance evidence. [P3]

That separation fits the proposed lower-memory policy search, but the paper does not supply poecraft2's native transition, projection or emitted-program correspondence. Preserve the existing complete original-root evaluator and proof scopes. A changed estimator cannot compensate for an omitted action or unrepresented trap.

## 4. Research path after the memory programme: recurrent continuation quality

The strongest earlier economic results changed recurrence, not just local acquisition spend. The Bow improvement retained acquisition cost while sharply increasing escape to the goal. The old reports remain historical and require their original runtime/profile qualifiers. [R09, H1]

The current private owner can query real compiler-declared entries and construct complete candidate programs, but clean one-goal-missing Rare entry service remains tied to execution modes, and the shortlist uses visits times remaining cost. Those are eligibility and heuristic choices, not proof that the top entries have the greatest achievable savings. [R11]

The next C5/post-incumbent question should follow one uninterrupted ordinary run: which useful *complete* change is missing, and is the obstruction entry eligibility, construction, checking, headroom, or final selection? If a proposed setup increases a later escape probability, evaluate the whole retry/return law. A goal-neutral setup can be essential. Do not repeat the economically negative captured-row C6147 candidate or rearm a refused graph merely because time passed.

This is queued, not automatically selected by the current packet. P0 may cheaply collect its missing full-duration observation.

## 5. Lower proofs need their own structural witness

The current canonical research record identifies a C5 auxiliary Scour/Alchemy/Fracture route near 405.369 that contains no Chaos. Further tightening only Chaos cannot remove that unchanged model winner. [R17]

Occupation-measure heuristics (Trevizan, Thiebaux and Haslum, ICAPS 2017) use probabilistic action-flow LP relaxations and offer a relevant way to reason about coupled projections. [P4] A future native experiment could retain one measured persistent occupancy/blocker/retention distinction or valid flow coupling that eliminates a demonstrably impossible optimistic route. It must preserve all native alternatives and charge cost once. More refined inequalities, a higher unused component or an incumbent-specific flow constraint is insufficient.

This is a serious research direction for exact closure, but it should not displace the now-visible ordinary memory barrier without a specific optimistic-policy witness and a representable native proof relation.

## 6. Execution-budget features remain a product decision

The user has asked about action limits. Mean primitive count, a hard execution horizon, and a required chance of finishing by a horizon are different problems. The existing exact count reward does not answer all three.

A deterministic ten-action controller and a geometric p=0.1 controller both have mean ten, yet their chances of finishing within five actions are 0 and 0.40951. Any fixed-policy distribution diagnostic must preserve joint duration/exit context for native macro programs; average duration alone loses the information. Keep this as a separately scoped feature/research decision rather than slipping a count penalty into original Chaos optimization. [R16, H1]

## 7. What would count as a substantial contribution

The potential contribution is not 'we invented selective expansion', 'we found a Schur complement', or 'we replaced JSON'. A stronger candidate is an empirically useful, authority-separated resource policy for an implicit SSP pipeline: optional construction can be deferred without losing complete executable witnesses, full-scope proof obligations or exact native entry/observation semantics, and the saved resources measurably enable better ordinary controllers.

That needs an implemented contract, counterexamples, matched end-to-end results and a real novelty comparison after the mechanism is known. This packet makes no claim that such a method is unprecedented.
