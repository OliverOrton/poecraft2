## Corrections to overbroad interpretations

**A fixed-programme cleanup rule is not a universal inability to retain metamods.** ProtectedRepeat and temporary-bench normalizers have particular exit contracts. Multimod/NoRoll setup inside a programme and explicit/native-authored policies demonstrate other contexts. The real limitation is the declared product grammar and its admitted exits, not a statement that the engine can never keep a metamod across operations.

**Singleton stopping does not destroy every other useful outcome.** The existing normalizer repeats only misses with a native retry-equivalence certificate; a surviving lock is an external exit, and unequal complete kernels exit too [S12]. Claude's F4 is a worthwhile specific hypothesis, not a measured universal failure. The earlier A4 pair-stop result remains a scoped negative. No repeated pair-stop trial is selected here.

**Admission is a major observed barrier, not a fully isolated cause of all late policy quality.** The private counters are cumulative transient work [S10]. They are not 1.39 billion simultaneously live edges. Parent max_reforge_work intentionally excludes that work; memory/other limits still exist. A 290-MB retained report does not prove a 1-GiB cap is false, because the refusal may concern prospective concurrent storage. Exclusive timers and first-refusal ownership are needed before a broader performance diagnosis.

**The reported 0.9745 action-not-applied mass is not automatically a no-matching-edge count.** Preserve the evaluator's actual categories and the named route-coverage failure; inspect the raw report for causality instead of paraphrasing all failure as missing routing.

**Multimod-in-goal is correct for a strict-clean deterministic finish, but not a reason to leave the old coverage-mode omission unresolved.** MM2 carries the original plan's separate coverage regression. Bench-able coverage tests are not natural-T1 benchmarks.

**Metadata flags are not complete laws.** Nonrenewal metadata is wrong when derived from a renewal-only table, but fixing it does not by itself qualify protected Annul/Harvest augment or make conditional preservation universal [S9].
