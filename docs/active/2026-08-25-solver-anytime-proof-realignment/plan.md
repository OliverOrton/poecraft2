## Objective

Turn the bounded product solver into a reliable proper-anytime planner and
then give it materially stronger admissible proof bounds:

1. reach and retain strong executable multi-goal policies early enough for
   Calculator/WASM use;
2. plan across intermediate exact-goal carrier shapes without imposing a
   fixed goal order or cleaning junk prematurely;
3. replace the current coarse carrier lower's broad free grants with several
   small, proved carrier/action pattern databases;
4. use those bounds to prune work and close more of the requested action
   envelope, rather than only improving a displayed number; and
5. retire the scheduler, incumbent, bounds, telemetry, test-authority, and
   documentation debt that makes each solver iteration risky.

The work is deliberately sequenced upper-first. Current five-T1 evidence
performs no incumbent operator-lower evaluation before its bounded finish, so
a stronger lower alone cannot prune that run. In contrast, the restored
carrier/action ordering candidate improved the independently evaluated upper
from `14454067.4260706` to `1562083.15196689`, while the older carrier-ladder
scheduler found an independently evaluated `87361.1690420501` policy under the
same request. The 1.56M result is a reproducible current-source qualification
target. The 87k artifact is a research anchor, not a deterministic current-
source baseline and not permission to restore an obsolete publication path.

## Product and proof boundaries

- No fixed subgoal order. Direct multi-slot jumps and all exact goal subsets
  remain legal. A route such as `0 -> 2 -> 5`, `0 -> 3 -> 4 -> 5`, or
  `3 -> 5` must compete on executable cost rather than hard-coded preference.
- Do not clean a carrier merely because junk exists. Cleanup is mandatory only
  when exact terminal success or the selected next action requires it. A
  protected side-targeted reforge may replace opposite-side junk directly.
- No action family may be filtered by an ordering score. Every requested and
  priced action remains an exact obligation until materialized, proved
  inapplicable, proved dominated by admissible authority, or disclosed as an
  unresolved bounded obligation.
- A restricted graph value never becomes a certified global lower while the
  requested action envelope is open. Independent patterns must cover every
  requested alternative or fall back locally.
- Unknown, unpriced, unsupported, or identity-mismatched proof shapes fall back
  to a weaker already-proved component. Missing proof coverage cannot become
  inapplicability, dominance, closure, or exactness.
- Candidate/coarse values, verified executable uppers, restricted-search
  values, and certified global lowers remain separate public and telemetry
  fields. A candidate failure cannot make the verified upper rise.
- Do not change caps as an optimization. A cap change requires a separate
  owner attribution, proof that the retained representation is useful, and a
  check that the next named cap will not immediately replace it.
- Intermediate gates run focused diagnostics only when needed. Run the
  appropriate complete native/release-WASM/nonvisual acceptance once at the
  end of the full plan. Rendered review remains Oliver's.

## Gate 1 — Action-envelope ledger and mechanic-family coverage

Create one typed `ActionEnvelopeLedger` as the authoritative lifecycle of each
carrier/operator obligation. Each obligation has exactly one current state:

- queued in a named scheduler lane;
- exact row complete;
- exact inapplicability proved;
- incumbent-dominated by named proof authority;
- rolled back transactionally after a named cap;
- omitted by explicit caller-selected action scope; or
- unresolved with a named stop owner.

The ledger must reuse existing `CarrierFacts`, `CarrierEffectSummary`,
`CarrierSuccessorEnvelope`, `ActionRefinementContract`, exact registry
legality, and exact option-kernel ownership. It must not invent parallel
action-name rules or turn possible destruction into guaranteed destruction.

Add focused current-semantics forced-winner controls for every major mechanic:

- Harvest reforge and augment, including target-tag behavior;
- Eldritch Chaos, Annul, and Exalt side intents;
- prefix/suffix protection and metamod-bearing followups;
- Essence;
- pure-reweight and added/forced-mod Fossils;
- Fracture, including miss recovery;
- Annul, Exalt, Scour, and rarity-changing primitives;
- temporary bench setup/cleanup and blocker routes; and
- explicit opt-in Imprint retry programs.

Each control must prove the family was registered, admitted on a legal
carrier, scheduled, materialized, selected under a disclosed synthetic price,
compiled, and independently exact-evaluated. A failure must identify which
stage lost it. Synthetic prices establish reachability/selection only and do
not become market evidence.

Audit the real product cases through the same ledger. Repair concrete current-
mechanic defects found by these controls. Do not force a family into a real
policy when exact evaluation correctly finds it worse.

Gate 1 passes when all required family controls produce proper executable
policies, current real cases disclose every requested family, and the ledger

## Gate 4 — Executable carrier/subgoal incumbent planner

Build a separate executable-upper planner over exact goal subsets and compact
carrier shapes. It is an incumbent generator, not a lower bound, pruning
rule, exactness claim, or restriction on the main solver.

Planner state must retain enough current carrier information to choose legal
and useful next actions:

- rarity and exact satisfied-goal mask;
- prefix/suffix occupancy and missing-goal side capacity;
- `blocked_mask` and junk/temporary/crafted debt;
- fractured goals and fractured junk;
- protection/metamod flags;
- influence and Eldritch side identity where relevant; and
- action-local preserved, destroyed, and created carrier properties.

Planner transitions may use exact small kernels and optimistic ordering scores
to search, but every proposed strategy must be constructed from real admitted
operations, compiled, and independently exact-evaluated before entering the
incumbent portfolio. Search must retain direct multi-goal outcomes and compete
across all useful masks. It must not commit globally to “get slot 1, then slot
2.”

Junk policy:

- exact terminal success requires all explicit affixes to be goal members;
- an intermediate junk modifier creates debt only when it blocks capacity,
  blocks a target group, prevents the next action, or remains at terminal;
- a protected or side-targeted reforge that can replace junk directly should
  compete without preliminary cleanup; and
- cleanup may be selected early when exact legality/probability makes it
  useful, not merely because junk exists.

Use the existing universal-cover predecessor/action/subset reconstruction as
one source of candidate mask sequences, but never treat its free-setup or
probability relaxation as executable. Add direct carrier-local reach and
probability/cost authority for the actual strategy.

Qualification:

- demonstrate a valid chained policy from a clean zero-to-five start;
- demonstrate partial three-to-five and four-to-five policies;
- include at least one direct multi-slot jump and one preservation/metamod
  route in focused fixtures;
- show that the planner omits unnecessary cleanup in the protected-replace
  witness;
- exact-evaluate every admitted planner incumbent with complete prices,
  success probability one, and zero off-policy mass; and
- improve first-verified-upper latency or fixed-window verified cost on at
  least one hard product case without degrading Gate 3 controls.

If the planner fails to improve any incumbent, retain only reusable typed
carrier projection if behavior-neutral and remove its product scheduling path.

## Gate 6 — Stronger carrier/action proof patterns

Add several small independent patterns rather than one unbounded cross
product. Initial candidates, measured and retained independently, are:

1. rarity × exact goal mask × prefix/suffix occupancy;
2. goal mask × `blocked_mask`/missing-side capacity;
3. goal mask × bounded junk/terminal-debt state;
4. goal mask × protection/metamod state;
5. goal mask × fractured-goal/identity state;
6. Eldritch dominant-side and tier state; and
7. influenced versus ordinary explicit-pool identity.

Use maxima first. Additive/cost-partitioned combinations are out of scope
unless a separate proof assigns disjoint immediate resource cost and prevents
double charging.

Replace the current carrier MDP's largest favorable grants where the exact
action contract proves a tighter relaxation:

- represent distributions or proved envelopes over successor goal subsets
  instead of “any useful hit grants every reachable missing goal”;
- choose one compatible favorable carrier/blocker shape per transition rather
  than independently maximizing every slot and summing incompatible maxima;
- retain only source goals that a compatible successor can preserve, while
  removing a goal only when absence is proved for every represented outcome;
- model nonterminal influence, protection, and Fracture successor identity
  instead of granting terminal success after immediate price;
- represent action-specific side occupancy, blocker, and junk effects;
- replace unknown productive shapes with local fallback rather than
  strengthening by assertion; and
- retain probability upper bounds, never averages or lower probabilities, in
  an admissible cost lower.

Replace one-step terminal debt with a compact debt-state MDP. It may charge
only unavoidable work and must still permit direct protected replacement
without fabricated Scour. Distinguish actions that merely are legal from
actions that can resolve a represented obstruction.

For authored fixed programs, build an optimistic program-cost automaton that
charges every unconditional primitive and a no-stronger minimum/expectation
over conditional branches. Continue using complete exact resource vectors for
state-local automatic options whose exact OptionKernel owns that quantity.

Extend strict-pattern coverage to rare source carriers and Eldritch automatic
side options. Remove the strict Eldritch guard only after every cheaper
admitted automatic option has a proved no-stronger row. Add pool identity for
influence where needed; otherwise retain local fallback.

Replace the unexplained 32-lift carrier cap with residual/convergence-aware
monotone solution or an appropriate finite SCC/LP solve. An interrupted
subsolution remains safe and reports its residual; it cannot claim
convergence.

Proof qualification for every retained pattern:

- `h(s) <= price(a) + E[h(S')]` for every materialized ordinary and automatic
  row in complete small fixtures;
- exact goal states remain zero and exact nonterminal debt stays nonnegative;
- adding actions cannot increase the optimal lower relaxation;
- increasing real action prices cannot decrease a fixed-action lower;
- unknown coverage falls back locally;
- native/WASM values agree; and
- current executable policies and exact-evaluated uppers remain unchanged
  before pruning consumers are enabled.

Add proof-bottleneck telemetry: pattern owner, minimizing action, fallback
reason, free-preservation/cleanup/identity grant, residual, and contribution.
Use counterexample-guided refinement: add state only for the assumption that
actually pins the start or unresolved-operator minimum.

Gate 6 must strengthen the independent five-T1 lower above
`36.4286171890906` and strengthen at least one dirty/protected partial-carrier
lower without losing proof, proper policy, or proportional wall/memory. A
numerical increase alone does not complete the milestone; Gate 7 owns the
required work consumer.
