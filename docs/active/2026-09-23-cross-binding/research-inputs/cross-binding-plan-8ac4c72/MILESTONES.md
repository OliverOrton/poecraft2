First compare a finite, identical native binding workload: existing cold path with its ordinary caches versus the new path. Include first-template cost, guard/mapping, all numeric work, scatter, memory and necessary checks. Use naturally observed distinct misses, not thousands of manufactured repeats.

Proposed work milestone: at least 25% less measured structural construction work AND at least 10% lower all-in cost for that relevant binding workload, or the same complete bound outputs within a previously binding native memory allowance. These are proposed experiment targets, not promised gains. Keep active timers, logical work and resident peaks distinct. A synthetic or perfect-symmetry-only result does not pass the heterogeneous requirement.

Only after a material native witness, run affected full-root cases serially. Prefer A3 or A4 based on the observed consumer, with B3/B4 as the other family and A5/B5 as suffix/context stress tests when touched. Do not manufacture a quality pass by extending the budget or using a prefilled entry as the original root. A throughput-only success may be worth retaining without meeting a new economic threshold; identify it correctly.

Time-to-same-completed-work or time-to-same-verified-cost is the appropriate timing target. A solve forced to finish at 240 seconds does not demonstrate no speedup merely because total time remains 240; nor does a small total-wall fluctuation prove one.
