modifier families have unequal weights, tier counts, prices and native effects.

## 2. Non-negotiable mathematical contract

Distinguish three identities:
1. structural template/control/recurrence shape;
2. native binding, including actual entry, family/member vectors, weights, full
   eligible/exclusion background, scope, observations and active support;
3. complete bound law/value/certificate and its original-root controller.

A fixed-target symmetry requires mapped native goal, legality, rewards and
successor laws to agree. A family equivariance can give
V_(g theta)(g s)=V_theta(s), not V_theta(g s)=V_theta(s) for asymmetric theta.
A common function C(theta) can produce different correct answers without merging
those states or their values.

For a finite proper bound controller:
  V_b=c_b+Q_b V_b.
Different coefficients may change both V_b and the best action. Recompute them
through existing native numerical owners. A template or old scalar cannot be an
upper certificate. Proper controllers from a restricted family supply uppers for
the original target; family-optimal values do not supply full-scope lowers or
retire omitted alternatives.

A sufficient acyclic specialization proof is induction over native leaves and
recurrence operations, with checked branch/support guards and complete output
mapping. A cyclic continuation needs matching bound equations and properness.
A few cold/parameterized comparisons test implementation; they do not alone prove
all allowed bindings. Unsupported or unknown guards use the existing cold path.

Ragged tiers are allowed binding data. Aggregate a member segment only where native
exclusions/effects/observations and relevant downstream meaning are identical;
otherwise preserve member leaves/scatter or split/refuse the shape. Equal total
weight does not preserve a distribution over differently observed members.
Support changes, including zero escape probability, can destroy properness.

Carry the inverse native role/action/resource map. If statuses change, update them;
if roles are re-canonicalized, update the map too. A relative 'missing role' cannot
remain bound to the wrong physical family. Hidden offer/checkpoint state, suffix
requirements, fracture/crafted flags and required-level observers remain semantic.

Retain all native outcomes, joint multi-goal hits, mandatory costs and real clean
terminal logic. Prefix-only does not allow unrequested suffix junk in these cases.
A partial/prefractured diagnostic entry is not free full-root evidence. Final
emitted policies use complete original-root evaluation, not a shape prediction.

Preserve native stored-coefficient/reconciliation meaning and deterministic order.
No relaxed epsilon, averaged probabilities, missing-mass normalization or value
copying by goal count. The exact-rational research examples are not native rules.
