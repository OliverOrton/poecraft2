# Native design: structure reuse without scalar-state merging

## 1. What is selected

A request-local, guarded structural template for ONE repeated native transition or continuation computation across different goal-family bindings. The concrete consumer is chosen from measured native cache misses in G0. Candidate structure, numerical parameters and publication evidence remain different objects.

This is not satisfied by a new `Role` struct wrapping a loop that still builds every concrete graph independently. It must bypass named structural work for a later binding. Conversely, do not build a universal graph-isomorphism engine before one native pair proves the concept.

### Existing owners

- `solver_reforge.cpp`: complete native reforge recurrence, exact preserved-base cache, canonical accumulation and actual-work attribution.
- `solver_options.cpp` and `solver_options_runtime_helpers.hpp`: native complete attempts, retry equivalence, option law/template comparison. Keep `same_complete_option_kernel` unchanged.
- `solver_calc.cpp` / `solver_calc_types.hpp`: actual pool/state/namespace, literal caches and owned storage.
- `solver_model.hpp`, `solver_options_helpers.hpp`, `solver_options_automatic.cpp`: existing goal-bound fixed programmes and transactional admission. Do not add the rejected pair-stop family.
- Native selected continuation/sparse policy owner and `solver_compile_contracts.hpp`: actual complete local decisions and safe original-root composition.
- Existing evaluator: independent coefficient/control/goal checking, not a template self-check alone.

The current V3 recurrence and existing exact cache are the baseline; a cold path with caches disabled is not an ordinary product comparison unless separately labelled as a correctness oracle.

## 2. A minimal representation

The names below are conceptual fields, not mandated new public classes:

**Shape:** fixed operation/choice topology; role-indexed exclusions and side-cap effects; relevant observation/control structure; output categories/ports; structural arithmetic dependencies or sparse equation topology; required support guards. Match the whole relevant dependency object, not a string/hash alone.

**Binding:** native family/member IDs, satisfying partitions, ragged native coefficient segments, complete background eligibility/exclusion data, actual item/control entry, action mapping, price/resource vector, native descriptor/runtime identity, numerical mode and active support. Numeric coefficients may differ. Shape-dependent branches do not become optional merely because they are inconvenient to match.

**Specialization workspace:** binding-local coefficient/vector values, actual successor scatter, mutable scratch, properness result, cumulative work debit and current cursor. Two interleaved bindings cannot overwrite one another's work.

**Bound output:** normal complete native rows or an actual complete selected controller, with its concrete semantic identity and original-root evaluation. No template handle alone constitutes policy availability.

## 3. Where parameters can and cannot live

A natural candidate is an unweighted role/exclusion dependency structure with native weight-sum and denominator slots. Two source states may share that structure although their available total or family weights differ. The native recurrence itself still decides which combinations and denominators are legal. Reuse the existing native arithmetic order and output contracts wherever possible.

Do not assume every `RollBucket` grouping is valid for every selected action or future observer. A tier segment can be reduced to its sum only when all members have identical relevant effects and successor observation category for this computation. Otherwise use member-valued leaves/scatter, separate subshapes, or cold construction. Exact goal member identity, required-level filters, offered choices and external group conflicts are common reasons not to aggregate.

Retaining a different coefficient vector is essential. A state with two attained roles but an expensive missing role cannot share a single value with one whose remaining role is cheap. Represent the value request as `(shape-state, binding/context)` or an equivalent parameterized function.

The physical mapping must remain available at every concrete action and successor. A static role map can persist through one local computation while statuses change. If a later design re-canonicalizes roles, the inverse map must update with it. A role called 'missing' is not a stable physical modifier ID.

## 4. Entry and output scope

The initial diagnostic should include the user's two-attained-prefix example, not exclusively protected-repeat on a suffix-only A4 item. It may turn out that the one-draw path is too cheap. In that case use the observed expensive continuation with the same relative structure, such as legally acquired fracture/protection context, without claiming the acquired item was free.

A narrowed selected-program representation is not a quotient for the entire MDP. Full-action optimality and lower evidence remain under their current owners. If the schema covers only a selected action family, adding another compared action requires its dependencies or a separately bound context.

Preserve the current native terminal predicate. Prefix-only requests in this corpus still require removal of unrelated explicit affixes. B3 cannot silently become 'the prefixes are present, any suffixes are fine.' Verify exact goal membership through native routines, not a target popcount or an old comment.

All positive outcomes survive to complete actual output: success, retry, loss, unchanged result, blocker/context variation or proper recovery. A stopped programme's expected resources and primitive actions include mandatory setup and cleanup. Parent/private IDs and local numerical vectors never transfer by position alone.

## 5. Placement and fallback

The matching hook must precede the structural work being saved. Let the first supported binding build and own the immutable shape. Later bindings validate the required contract and specialize it. If validation requires complete independent enumeration each time, report that cost; do not call a post-hoc comparison an optimization.

An explicit finite family of candidate role permutations is sufficient initially; three requested prefix roles produce at most six permutations. Do not hardcode fire/cold/lightning, base metadata, case hashes or historical node IDs into runtime logic. Native structural requirements should select eligible contexts.

Failed/unknown guard -> existing cold path, with a compact reason. Numerical/support change -> recompute or refuse that bound instance, not publish the previous binding's answer. An all-zero useful escape probability is not accepted because another instance was proper.

Prefer an owner-local helper/type over more global `SolveWork::Impl` state. Avoid strings or full JSON canonicalization in the hot path. No change to `pc_solve_progress` ABI, public strategy vocabulary or WASM compilation flags is needed to represent private structural reuse.

## 6. Accounting and determinism

Charge template storage once through the actual owner, plus every active binding's coefficient/output vectors and simultaneous growth scratch. Charge detached in-flight ownership until release. Eviction does not invalidate a currently executing specialization. Cancellation cannot install partial outputs; releasing bytes cannot refund consumed work.

Actual saved operations and V1-equivalent logical work are different metrics. Follow the existing work contract for a complete specialized law; do not reset the logical debit on a template hit or change the 50M cap. If logical limits prevent extra search despite a CPU saving, report faster same-work completion rather than claim broader search.

Shape permutations can change floating accumulation order. Preserve established canonical ordering; any necessary numerical-order change requires explicit qualification under the existing acceptance contract, never an increased tolerance. Native rational identities in MATHEMATICS.md do not imply bitwise equality for an arbitrary new floating implementation.

## 7. No unnoticed architecture substitution

A symbolic coefficient evaluator is not an independent native checker. A general-purpose function already parameterized by weights is not automatically a reusable compiled instance. A cache of identical outputs is not heterogeneous sharing. A transferred fixed action policy without reoptimization is weaker than a shared decision problem.

Record the exact level achieved, identify saved operations, and use that evidence to justify further work. No generic recipe library, all-model symbolic solution, dynamic full-state quotient or numerical certificate redesign is part of this programme.
