## 2. Contracts to preserve

### Evidence ladder

Track these distinct facts for the same original-root candidate:

1. A finite internal estimate exists.
2. Its selected policy is complete enough to materialize an actual graph.
3. Independent graph checking is in progress or has completed, with explicit acceptance/refusal.
4. A compatible checked graph/certificate bundle has an owner and is actually selectable.
5. The optional service is eligible and then actually admitted.
6. Bounded Finish publishes a usable, accepted result.

The sequence is a diagnostic model, not a requirement to add a second production state machine. Records must identify the actual owner—candidate continuation, retained portfolio, staged verifier, output bundle or final publication—rather than treating a scalar or status string as ownership.

### Three noninterference obligations

**Before service starts:** at identical fixed native work boundaries, service-off and requested-but-waiting executions must agree on ordinary semantic state, completed rows and probability payloads, normal scheduling decisions, proof use, candidate lineage, and owned artifact identities. Exclude wall time, service-specific status and bounded observational counters from the equality projection. A declared tiny metadata allocation is not hidden private construction; account for it, and test cap-edge behavior separately. Do not exclude substantive differences simply to make the comparison pass.

**At ownership transitions:** for an unchanged semantic request, moving or staging an accepted bundle must not erase its availability or invalidate its graph/certificate references. A historical monotone best cost is not proof that this remains true. Actual invalidation follows the existing compatibility contract; an append-only graph generation does not automatically invalidate a compatible prefix, and a changed semantic identity must not be accepted merely because its cost is attractive.

**After admission:** optional work may consume time and resources and may change subsequent search opportunity. Do not promise an identical whole-run trajectory. It must preserve the best compatible checked artifact available on entry unless a genuinely better compatible artifact replaces it, charge all work and live memory, yield cooperatively, and respect Finish/caps/cancellation. Bounded Finish and cancellation have different contracts: cancellation may intentionally return no strategy and must release work; do not change it into publication.

No root-only checked value becomes an arbitrary statewise continuation, a positive MDP lower, an action-retirement certificate or an exact-closure claim. A later improved upper may affect existing valid bound-driven decisions; that is a post-admission effect to explain, not an excuse for a pre-admission difference. Existing numerical acceptance, properness, full probability mass, paid recovery, native entry validation, original target and price obligations remain unchanged. [S3, S5]
