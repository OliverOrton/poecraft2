## Decision

Retain the default-off occupancy-order diagnostic and its matched A5 negative result. Do not rerun O variants. Complete the missing **S first-policy selector measurement**, then deliver **G: one genuine native progress-preserving programme family in the existing experimental finder**. These are two separately reported deliverables, not competing branches. A negative, inactive or censored S observation does not cancel G. A successful G result does not prove Current's seed heuristic was defective.

Current's selection behaviour stays unchanged during this programme. S is a passive diagnostic and, when existing complete support permits, a frozen-candidate economic comparison. A live S behaviour change is a possible separately specified successor, not an automatic second algorithm treatment here. G is the selected runtime implementation.

The original clean goal, actual root, price scope, native programme permissions, probability laws and computational limits remain fixed. Current remains the default. The finder remains experimental unless a later explicit promotion is justified.

## What completion means

S must have explicit instrumentation and coverage/status, whether or not it produces a reversal. G must have an actual generated, checked controller that reaches a dirty intermediate item, preserves a declared requested holding through a native programme, and reaches the same strict final goal with all acquisition and recovery paid. A side-aware classifier, additional eligible counts, or another Scour/Alchemy reset is not that deliverable.

Engineering capability, economic improvement over the old finder, economic competitiveness with Current, and product qualification are different claims. Keep each one separate. Unknown or capped candidates are not expensive candidates. An unavailable old donor provenance is not permission to manufacture boundaries, and is not a reason to stop new programme construction.
