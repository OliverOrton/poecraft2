# Mathematical and causal contracts

These are conditional derivations and diagnostic specifications. They are not new native measurements, rigorous outward-rounded numerical bounds, general convergence theorems or novelty claims. The existing native evaluator and numerical-acceptance contract remain authoritative for returned policies.

## 1. The three hypotheses have different variables

O changes the order in which a fixed eligible carrier set is served. S changes a candidate's initial row-choice heuristic. G changes which finite controllers can be constructed. Equal final cost after one O comparison does not imply invariance under S or G. Nor does a G improvement identify an O/S bias.

A valid O ablation need not begin with an economically certified carrier. But without that prior witness it answers the weaker direct-intervention question, not the stronger explanation that a known good continuation was suppressed. That distinction should be made before the experiment and preserved afterward.

Mutually exclusive classification also does not identify feature prevalence. If blocked is assigned before high-progress, `count(high_progress_label)` excludes `blocked AND high_progress`. The new diagnostic records both properties and its population denominator. No old count is retroactively reinterpreted.

## 2. S's union-credit counterfactual

At a nonterminal source s, define D(s) by the actual native debt and G(s) by native satisfied-slot accounting. Under the real first-policy gate define events

E_D = {D(S') < D(s)},   E_G = {G(S') > G(s)}.

The proposed progress mass is P(E_D union E_G), not P(E_D)+P(E_G). It satisfies

P(E_D union E_G) >= P(E_D).

This preserves old cleanup-advance events and adds acquisition-only events. For an observed-choice group, the existing heuristic counts one group if any permitted post-observation alternative advances; its probability is credited once regardless of the number of alternatives. This is still a heuristic, not a selected-policy law.

Larger progress mass can reduce `cost/progress`, but the overall row key also includes class, pending routes, true-goal mass and ties. It does not follow that the winning row changes, that a complete candidate exists, or that its true cost improves. In particular, if two progress-class rows have different pending counts, that earlier key coordinate can keep the original winner.

The gate depends on existence of an output candidate object, not its independent verification. Applying the new predicate until the first checked graph would be a different treatment with a larger domain. This packet measures the original domain only.

## 3. A count-to-terminal proxy is not a cost-to-go value

For an ordinary nonterminal item at the requested rarity and with valid distinct-goal accounting, D=max(1,m+k-2g). Thus m=4, k=6, g=3 gives the same value as an empty Rare: 4. This equality is already in the canonical mathematics.

It is possible for that dirty state to be cheap or expensive. The effect depends on the native continuation, not just its count. Thayer and Ruml's separation of search distance from cost is relevant precedent; their deterministic search experiments do not prove a particular ordering wins here. [P1]

## 4. Paid acquisition, cleanup and loss: a complete toy model

Let r be an acquisition state and d a dirty holding. This is an invented finite model, not a native crafting formula.

At r, pay a, then reach goal with probability p, d with probability q, or r with probability 1-p-q. At d, pay b, then reach goal with probability u, lose the holding and return to r with probability v, or return to d with probability t, with u+v+t=1. All costs are finite and nonnegative. Take q>0 and 1-t>0; require an actual reachable goal escape (equivalently Delta>0 for this two-state reachable model):

Delta = p(1-t) + q u.

The complete Bellman equations are

```
(p+q) V_r - q V_d = a
-v V_r + (1-t) V_d = b
```

and therefore

V_r = [a(1-t)+q b] / [p(1-t)+q u].

For p=0, q=1/5, a=2, u=1/2, v=1/4, t=1/4, b=1:

V_r=17 and V_d=7.

The acquisition prefix alone costs a/q=10. The remaining seven includes cleanup, losing the holding and reacquisition. A direct cost-100 route loses to the complete dirty-progress policy. Change b to 200 and the dirty policy becomes expensive despite identical occupancy/progress labels. Setting p=u=0 creates a closed recurrent class; local fragment exit does not give global goal reachability.

For p>0 some trajectories finish without visiting d. The expected post-first-entry contribution is then weighted by the actual first-entry probability, not by raw visits or by assuming every path acquires the dirty state.

These equations are a special case of the existing full-controller/first-exit mathematics, not permission to replace a multi-context native calculation with two scalars.

## 5. Preservation and initiation are programme-specific

Let H identify requested native holdings. For a complete programme o and every concrete state x in its claimed initiation domain I_o, a true preservation statement is

P(H remains satisfied at the declared complete exit | x,o) = 1,

or a stronger whole-path property if that is actually promised. It must follow from a sound native contract or complete law; a MAY-survive descriptor or one materialized representative does not prove it.

Preservation does not imply feasibility of the final target. The remaining side can still be blocked, unavailable or expensive. Loss that is possible must be explicit, with paid recovery and no probability deletion. A programme leaving a temporary lock needs a legal continuation for that exit; unconditional cleanup is not safe when the lock may already have been removed.

The options literature formalizes distinct initiation, internal policy and termination roles. The implementation must preserve its own native observation/commitment boundary; general option-interruption results do not authorize jumping into a mandatory programme. [P2]

## 6. Scoped compilation preserves the controller, not arbitrary dependencies

Suppose each admitted native programme occurrence compiles into an ordinary graph fragment whose operations, observations, paid resources and all exit continuations correspond to the requested programme for every reached member. If fragment edges preserve the declared entry/exit discipline and hidden control, concatenating these correspondences yields the same selected-controller execution law.

This requires full programme permission. An internal action's appearance in one permitted occurrence does not make it selectable at another decision. Untrusted graph metadata cannot establish that correspondence. Native construction supplies the binding and the compiler's occurrence manifest; preparation validates it before independent evaluation.

A success terminal is valid only through the effective original native goal predicate. Default-edge syntax can erase an authored condition; preserve the existing failure test. Coverage predicates may select construction stages but cannot authorize success.

## 7. Returned upper authority

For a complete original-root policy pi that is legal, fully priced and proper under the actual request, its native evaluation supplies J_pi under the existing numerical contract. In the exact mathematical model,

V*(root) <= J_pi(root).

Taking the minimum among currently owned compatible checked artifacts preserves feasibility. The historical best scalar cannot replace a lost graph/certificate. Approximate role features and a small search frontier affect which candidates are attempted; they do not prove optimality or confer a lower bound.

No candidate's proper local fragment proves the whole controller proper. Two fragments that each leave their local domain can alternate forever without a goal. Full original-root checking remains mandatory.

## 8. First-hit economics is optional explanatory evidence

For a proper finite controller with complete native state/control and a legitimate observable stopping time tau (first requested coverage at declared complete decisions), splitting the nonnegative accumulated cost gives

J_pi(root) = E[cost before tau] + E[J_pi(X_tau)].

The prefix must match the original controller. An old graph lacking trusted complete-decision declarations cannot be cut arbitrarily to apply this diagnostic. Missing data remains unknown. A new native compiler may supply such provenance for its own candidates; any resulting stopped graph is diagnostic only and cannot be offered as a solution to the clean target.

A small post-full-coverage bill does not rule out repeatedly discarding valuable partial progress earlier. A large bill is not a bound on avoidable savings. The current programme does not require reconstructing the old missing provenance before G can proceed.

## 9. What the abstract checks establish

The companion exact-rational checks exercise event union, key precedence, exclusive-stratum interpretation, the finite two-state equations and improper-cycle counterexample, and proposed evidence statuses. They do not emulate PoE, validate native initiation, assert real S activation or predict a G speed/cost improvement. Their independence is at the mathematical/specification level only.
