## 4. Two qualifications to that audit

### Exclusive strata change the interpretation of 50

J1's categories are chosen in this order:

```
full requested coverage + extras
else side capacity obstructed OR missing-goal blocker
else at least two goals + extras
else no extras
else other
```

An item with three valuable goals, extra affixes and an obstruction enters the second category, not the third. Therefore the reported 50 is the number of changed positions in the residual high-progress-dirty category. It is not the total number of high-progress dirty items tested. The 2,534 obstructed positions may overlap that property. Also, the observations count changed positions across frozen epochs before sample deduplication, not 2,612 unique states. Zero full-coverage-extra changes does not prove no such state was discovered, served or used. [R6]

Future diagnostic totals must use overlapping property masks and explicit denominators, while retaining the old category as historical data. Do not rewrite the old counts into a new schema.

### Stopping after O was not an unauthorized omission of two live treatments

The previous prompt selected one O/S/G runtime implementation and explicitly stopped after its bounded negative. Codex correctly did not launch S and G automatically after O. The shortfall was the weaker-than-declared branch selection and incomplete measurement/status of the other requested channel, not a general obligation to implement all three despite the scope rule. [R3]

This packet fixes the planning ambiguity: S observation is mandatory; G construction is separately selected. S cannot monopolize the programme or cancel G merely because it has no event. Current S behaviour is not changed by this authorization.
