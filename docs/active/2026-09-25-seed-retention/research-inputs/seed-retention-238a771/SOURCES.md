## Primary research

### P1 — Jordan Thayer and Wheeler Ruml (2009), Using Distance Estimates in Heuristic Search

https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICAPS/article/view/13390

Primary conference page/abstract reviewed. It distinguishes search distance from cost-to-go and studies their use in deterministic heuristic search. The present relevance is the distinction between progress proxies and economics, not a transferred improvement guarantee or an authorization to use unproven lower bounds.

### P2 — Richard S. Sutton, Doina Precup and Satinder Singh (1999), Between MDPs and semi-MDPs: A framework for temporal abstraction in reinforcement learning

https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0004370299000521

Publisher abstract/search content was available; direct page open returned 403 during this review. Used only as established temporal-abstraction/options precedent, not as a proof of the project's specific macro implementation or licence to interrupt mandatory programmes. The native programme/price/goal/properness contracts must be established independently.
