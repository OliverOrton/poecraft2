## Primary research consulted

**P01.** Jordan Thayer and Wheeler Ruml, *Using Distance Estimates in Heuristic Search*, ICAPS 2009. [Official paper record](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICAPS/article/view/13390). Cost-to-go and distance-to-go answer different questions. This supports measuring first-witness construction separately from crafting economics; it does not prove the existing debt heuristic good or bad on PoE.

**P02.** H. Brendan McMahan, Maxim Likhachev and Geoffrey Gordon, *Bounded Real-Time Dynamic Programming: RTDP with Monotone Upper Bounds and Performance Guarantees*, ICML 2005. [Authors' institutional record](https://publications.ri.cmu.edu/bounded-real-time-dynamic-programming-rtdp-with-monotone-upper-bounds-and-performance-guarantees). Relevant to selective service with separately justified bounds. The current solver already has selective machinery. No convergence theorem is imported into a modified native scheduler without its own premises.

**P03.** Dimitri Bertsekas, *Biased Aggregation, Rollout, and Enhanced Policy Improvement for Reinforcement Learning*, 2019. [Author paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.02426). Relevant to complete continuation improvement from a base policy; approximation results are not permission to transfer native values or merge item states.
