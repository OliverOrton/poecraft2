## Primary research

### P1 — Cyclic partial-graph heuristic search

Eric A. Hansen and Shlomo Zilberstein, *LAO*: A heuristic search algorithm that finds
solutions with loops*, Artificial Intelligence 129(1–2),35–62,2001,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0004-3702(01)00106-0).
The primary publisher abstract was available in search; direct full-page opening
failed. Only its stated high-level result is used: heuristic search can find cyclic
MDP solutions without evaluating the entire state space. No detailed convergence
conditions are imported from unread text and no new LAO* implementation is proposed.

### P2 — Proposal and certificate separation

Krishnendu Chatterjee, Tim Quatmann, Maximilian Schäffeler, Maximilian Weininger,
Tobias Winkler and Daniel Zilken, *Fixed Point Certificates for Reachability and
Expected Rewards in MDPs*,2025, [arXiv2501.11467v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.11467v1).
Read abstract, introduction, finite-MDP/reward semantics and certificate framing.
Relevant as precedent for separately checked witnesses and qualitative support.
Its explicit finite-model correctness is not automatically native abstraction or
coefficient correspondence here. No interval issuer/new bound mechanism selected.

### P3 — Recent trajectory-driven policy improvement, not a plug-in guarantee

Yuchao Li, Fei Chen, Yingke Li, Chuchu Fan and Dimitri Bertsekas, *On-Line Policy
Iteration with Trajectory-Driven Policy Generation*,2026,
[arXiv2604.15004v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.15004v1).
Reviewed abstract/overall problem framing: mainly deterministic finite-horizon,
fixed-root consistency, with discussion of stochastic counterpart. It does not
supply a drop-in proof for native cyclic stochastic controllers or justify sampling
one trajectory instead of complete support. This programme makes no novelty or
algorithm adoption claim from it.
