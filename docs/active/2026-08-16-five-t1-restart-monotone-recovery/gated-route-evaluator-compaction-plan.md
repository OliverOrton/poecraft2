# Proof-Gated Route And Operation-Graph Compaction

**Status: selected 2026-08-17. Gate 0 in progress.**

Parent: [Five-T1 Restart-Monotone Strategy Recovery](README.md)

## Objective

Make the priced-base five-natural-T1 candidate materially smaller to evaluate
and cooperatively convert without changing its policy, mechanics, prices,
probabilities, observation authority, or publication truth. The first target
is the measured state-local/direct-row tail: non-shared goal-progress-gated
reforges compile through local retry routers, which prevents the exact
evaluator from selecting the existing compact gated kernel and leaves full
physical outcome rows in the post-partition conversion.

Fracture selection is a separate diagnostic lane. It may expose a policy-
quality defect, but no Fracture preference or Bellman change is authorized by
this plan without an exact failing witness.

Parent successor Gates 4-8, release WASM, the priced primary publication run,
and the full acceptance pipeline remain closed until Witness B is
independently materializable.

## Invariants

1. Compact evaluation is an implementation choice beneath the existing exact
   compiled-strategy semantics. It must reproduce the full ungated evaluator's
   transitions, absorptions, mass, costs, hashes, flows, and attribution.
2. A local route may use the gated kernel only after proving that it observes
   exactly the gated contract: goal termination, exact partial progress, and a
   canonical zero-progress retry basin. Any extra observation, modifier offer,
   ambiguous target, cycle, or failure falls back to the full distribution.
3. Compiler annotations, if introduced, are internal proof provenance. They
   cannot grant authority without structural validation by the evaluator.
4. Hashes remain bucket selectors only; complete behavior equality is the
   sharing authority.
5. Operation-region sharing includes the complete emitted recipe: operation,
   accounting roles, goal-progress condition, retry continuation behavior,
   partial-progress continuation, and failure/default behavior. Expected cost
   is omitted when members disagree; it never distinguishes execution.
6. Recursive/cyclic retry continuations require a fixed-point behavior
   partition. No one-pass key may assume its own conclusion.
7. Telemetry is cap-accounted and behavior-neutral. It must not change values,
   bounds, termination, policy/action hashes, selected actions, graph output,
   or cap classifications except where an explicitly measured representation
   change moves the resource boundary.
8. Fracture diagnostics use the canonical sparse-Q authority. No forced
   Fracture result may publish or influence ordinary policy selection.
9. The one-GiB native/WASM budget, checked ten-million transition cap,
   minimum-expected-cost objective, prices, action envelope, and mechanics are
   unchanged.
10. Certification remains fail closed. The six-node Chaos renewal remains the
    publication until the preferred candidate independently exact-evaluates
    and reconciles.

## Gate 0 - Behavior-Neutral Attribution

Add aggregate, cap-accounted compiler/evaluator telemetry sufficient to choose
the smallest exact change:

- graph nodes and edges by policy router, local gated router, operation,
  special option, and fixed infrastructure;
- gated versus ungated operation rows by action, including exact outcomes,
  routed/direct transitions, row bytes, and active time;
- local gated routes accepted/rejected for compact evaluation, with bounded
  reason counts;
- operation behavior-signature multiplicities and row reuse blocked only by
  differing operation-node identity;
- deterministic route depth/traversal and trace payload; and
- Fracture row Q versus the selected Q, successor-value status, revisit count,
  and final non-selection reason on representative carriers.

Add focused resource-stopped and bounded tests. Apart from diagnostics, the
reference graphs, values, hashes, actions, exact-evaluation results, and cap
boundaries must remain unchanged. Use Witness A and the checked ten-million
Witness B only after the focused native tests pass.

Exit Gate 0 with measured attribution. If non-shared gated rows do not own a
material share of the direct-row tail, stop and update the handoff instead of
implementing the hypothesized fast path.

## Gate 1 - Structurally Proved Local Gated Kernel

Generalize compact kernel selection across a deterministic local gated route.
The evaluator must prove, from compiled structure and the canonical gated
retry state, that:

1. the operation is an exact goal-progress-gated reforge;
2. every zero-progress outcome follows the local route to one exact retry
   operation selected for the canonical retry basin;
3. positive partial progress returns to the exact policy router;
4. goal outcomes remain terminal under the existing goal authority; and
5. no condition observes modifier offers or state outside the gated contract.

Keep the full ungated path as an oracle. Add positive direct-repeat and local-
retry tests, plus negative tests for extra observations, wrong retry targets,
cycles, and authored lookalikes. Compare exact rows, values, costs, hashes,
flows, and failure mass.

## Gate 2 - Complete-Behavior Region Sharing

Only if Gate 0 shows material node-identity or routing duplication after Gate
1, construct a fixed-point partition of emitted executable behaviors before
the master policy route DAG. Share operation/local-route regions only when the
complete behavior contract in the invariants matches. Then let the existing
decision-DAG hash-consing collapse newly identical parent subtrees.

Prove direct-repeat, differing retry action, partial-progress action change,
Fracture, Imprint, observed Unveil, protected repeat, temporary Bench repeat,
and fail-closed default controls. This gate is skipped if Gate 1 removes the
material evaluator owner without requiring compiler-region sharing.

## Gate 3 - Fracture Selection Audit

Trace exact primitive-Fracture rows through admission, Q evaluation,
reconsideration, sparse-policy selection, and final policy capture. Add a
forced-Fracture diagnostic oracle on representative one- and two-progress
states, but do not publish it.

If canonical Q proves Fracture cheaper and the ordinary policy still rejects
it, repair only that exact selection/revisit defect with a focused failing
witness. If Fracture is genuinely more expensive under the current economy
and continuation values, retain zero selection and document the result.

the one-GiB and five-minute projections. Restore checked caps immediately.

Witness A must remain independently exact at `624800.9519118543`, success one,
zero off-policy mass, and paired-default-only. If Witness B becomes
materializable, continue to conversion cooperativity and then return to the
parent successor plan. Otherwise stop with the precise remaining owner.

## Gate 5 - Conversion Cooperativity

If Witness B reaches or materially approaches closed conversion, split the
owning partition/conversion loops into resumable work items without changing
ordering or numeric authority. No public native/WASM work step may exceed 250
ms in the frozen cases. Whole-run checkpoint/replay remains out of scope.

## Acceptance And Stop Conditions

Stop and update `HANDOFF.md` if:

1. compact evaluation cannot prove the local gated route structurally;
2. the full and compact exact oracles differ in any transition, probability,
   value, hash, flow, attribution, or failure mass;
3. gated rows are not a material owner of the direct-row tail;
4. behavior sharing requires weakening state-local or observation authority;
5. Witness A changes value, success, off-policy mass, or paired defaults;
6. Fracture work changes the policy without a canonical cheaper-Q witness;
7. a higher probe is not projected below both memory and five minutes;
8. a mechanics ambiguity appears; or
9. Witness B remains unmaterializable after the selected exact representation
   work.

Do not build release WASM, run web acceptance, the Warlord/automatic matrix,
the priced primary publication run, or `scripts/test.ps1` before Witness B
closes. If it closes, amend this plan with the proportional downstream gate
before running it.
