## 4. User action limits still need a product contract

Three different questions remain: constrain expected primitive executions; optimize a policy that stops after a hard number of primitive actions; or require a given probability of success within a budget. Properness or a scalarized C+λN proposal supplies none of the other contracts automatically.

A useful first deliverable would describe completion probability by selected primitive-action budgets for **already verified** controllers, using actual primitive/mandatory-program counting and complete exit context. That is diagnostic, not an optimizer or a new bound on all policies. Any future hard-cap problem must specify what happens on failure at the cap and whether retries/restarts consume the budget.
