## Stop and retry discipline

Ordinary test-driven repair of the selected interface is not a failed research variant. Stop an unsafe arm immediately for unresolved mass, target, scope, numerical or ownership correspondence. At most two substantive failed same-target repair variants without a new causal premise. Do not repeat long runs to chase a favourable score. A coherent diagnostic interface and precise negative are valid retained outcomes; an unconsumed abstraction scaffold is not.
