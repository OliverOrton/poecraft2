# Compact Better-Policy Certification Result

**Status: complete and accepted (2026-08-22).**

Parent: [plan](plan.md)

## Result

The Imprint-disabled Allflame four-natural-T1 Conquest Lamellar now publishes
the cheaper direct runtime policy at `2823.050846721888` chaos instead of the
previous `3759.9822404728984` fallback. The result remains correctly bounded:
the coarse solver estimated `2889.7687877196995`, so its 66.718-chaos mismatch
with the independently evaluated executable graph invalidates that coarse
lower as publication authority. The public lower is therefore zero and no
exact claim is made.

The compiler now shares ordinary primitive policy regions by their complete
emitted behavior: exact operation JSON, accounting roles, and the common
return to the master policy route. Transient operator indices are not runtime
behavior. A gated-capable reforge with zero actual retry mass is an ordinary
act-then-return region and may share on that authority. Product Fracture,
positive-mass gated repeats, observation-owned choices, state-local options,
and compound fixed options keep their existing stronger or local identities.

This reduces the controlled direct graph from 3,208 nodes / 7,139 edges /
1,356 policy regions to 570 nodes / 1,690 edges / 33 policy regions. Primitive
regions fall from 1,333 to 10, and strategy JSON falls from about 3.00 MB to
806 KB. Exact pair discovery is still real carrier behavior rather than graph
duplication: it closes at 162,829 raw pairs and refines to 43,833 behavioral
pairs.

Calculator's optional direct-certification allowance is consequently raised
from 5,000 to the measured 200,000-pair boundary. This is not a cap-only fix:
the emitted graph is 82% smaller by nodes and 76% smaller by edges first. Once
the direct graph independently proves proper, zero-off-policy, completely
priced execution and improves the verified portfolio, the product's nonzero
optional-proof mode publishes it bounded without launching a redundant strict
lift. Native callers that leave the allowance at zero retain historical
exhaustive strict-lift behavior.

## Remaining boundary

This milestone fixes compact certification and publishes the cheaper policy
when Imprint is explicitly excluded. It does not make the default
Imprint-enabled solve select that policy: the included Imprint family still
ends at its known program-work refusal and publishes the verified 3,759.976
upper. Further work should separately choose between exact Imprint recovery,
making a non-Imprint candidate compete under an open Imprint obligation, or
reducing WASM direct-evaluator latency and maximum cooperative work-item time.
