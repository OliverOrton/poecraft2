## Gate 0 - Dominance proof

For an Imprint prefix `p` and terminal primitive `a`, prune `p > a` before
exact program evaluation only when:

1. `a` is legal at every positive-mass carrier after `p`;
2. `a` has one identical exact destructive-reforge signature at every prefix
   carrier; and
3. an already evaluated shorter program in the same admitted priced grammar
   owns that signature with componentwise-no-greater primitive counts.

Then the shorter program's complete canonical outcome distribution equals
`(p > a)` and consumes a componentwise subset of its nonnegative resources.
It therefore dominates the prefixed terminal word under the current and every
other nonnegative economy. This includes setup-dependent cycles such as
`Fossil > Chaos > Chaos`, where direct Chaos is illegal at the Magic
checkpoint but `Fossil > Chaos` already owns the exact Rare Chaos kernel.
Persistent carrier features such as Fracture, influence, locks, corruption,
or Eldritch state change the reforge signature and prevent this prune.

The proof may increment existing exact-distribution dominance telemetry, but
must not claim price closure or avoid the existing cooperative legality and
owned-memory checkpoints.
