## Success is not a rename

A role descriptor, generic loop or template hit alone does not establish a smaller state space, less native work, a cheaper policy, or exactness. Require a working consumer and either a materially better verified heterogeneous-case policy or a measured reusable-work reduction at equal coverage/quality. Preserve those outcomes as separate claims.
