# Primary research

## P1 — Ravindran and Barto, Relativized Options: Choosing the Right Transformation (ICML 2003)

[Ravindran and Barto, Relativized Options: Choosing the Right Transformation (ICML 2003)](https://www.cse.iitm.ac.in/~ravi/papers/ICML03.pdf)

Shared relative option representation and state-dependent action maps; exact homomorphism conditions versus heuristic approximate transformation. Its discounted learning experiment is not native SSP qualification.

## P2 — Junges et al., Parameter Synthesis for Markov Models: Covering the Parameter Space

[Junges et al., Parameter Synthesis for Markov Models: Covering the Parameter Space](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.07993)

Parameterized transition models distinguish structure from numerical valuation. No general symbolic parameter-synthesis engine is selected.

## P3 — Bonet and Geffner, General Policies, Serializations, and Planning Width

[Bonet and Geffner, General Policies, Serializations, and Planning Width](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.08033)

Generalized subgoal sketches as search guidance. Classical termination/width conclusions do not establish stochastic properness or economic optimality here.

## P4 — Yang et al., PG3: Policy-Guided Planning for Generalized Policy Generation (IJCAI 2022)

[Yang et al., PG3: Policy-Guided Planning for Generalized Policy Generation (IJCAI 2022)](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2022/650)

Distinguish a policy used to guide planning from one used as an unverified executable answer. No training or learned planner in this implementation.
