## P1 — Rollout as policy improvement (primary abstract)
D. Bertsekas, *Biased Aggregation, Rollout, and Enhanced Policy Improvement for
Reinforcement Learning* (2019), [arXiv1910.02426](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.02426).
Author abstract reviewed: relation between a known policy's cost and rollout/
policy improvement. The approximate aggregation method and its guarantees are
not imported into poecraft2; this packet's finite-controller algebra is derived
explicitly and tested independently.
