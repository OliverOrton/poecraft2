inputs rather than call the full dirty-root programme or copy its large coroutine.
Use the existing solve/publication task owner at a safe post-verification boundary.

## L3 — Qualify and decide activation

Compare source-matched default-off versus enabled controls with original root,
prices, action scope, time/work/memory limits and compact host transport unchanged.
Reuse compatible completed evidence. Confirm a claimed economic/timing result with
one counterbalanced pair when host/load or near-threshold evidence warrants it.

A 20% lower original-root cost against the strongest compatible fresh baseline is
a **material-quality target**, not a correctness threshold or predicted outcome.
Report and preserve every strict verified improvement with its all-in expense;
do not discard a useful smaller result solely for missing that target. Default
promotion additionally requires preservation of A4's known final policy and A5's
first-policy/Finish guarantees, a second legitimate structural context, and actual
source-matched worker qualification. Otherwise retain only a justified diagnostic
capability or return an explicit activation decision; do not silently enable it.

A3/B3/B4/B5 are targeted generalization witnesses, not a mandatory campaign. A
same-shape negative with different native binding must still be rejected or choose
its own best action. Keep old wide Ring/Bow/Amulet profiles separate.
