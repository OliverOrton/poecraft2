## Primary literature checked in this review

**P1** Paul Gainer, Ernst Moritz Hahn, Sven Schewe. *Incremental Verification of Parametric and Reconfigurable Markov Chains* (2018), [full HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1804.01872v1). Inspected stable/volatile partition and reconfiguration conditions, state elimination and reward extension. The previous audit could only access an abstract; full text was available here. Relevant precedent for repeated model checking, not proof of native dependency correspondence or empirical savings.

**P2** Robert Givan, Thomas Dean, Matthew Greig. *Equivalence notions and model minimization in Markov decision processes*, Artificial Intelligence 147 (2003), 163–223, [publisher](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0004370202003764). Publisher abstract/bibliography inspected. Broad all-action minimization precedent; own conditional fixed-policy argument is given in MATHEMATICS, without claiming this paper's full theorem was re-audited.

**P3** Felipe Trevizan, Sylvie Thiébaux, Patrik Haslum. *Occupation Measure Heuristics for Probabilistic Planning*, ICAPS 27 (2017), 306–315, [proceedings](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICAPS/article/view/13840). Official abstract/metadata inspected here; U2 reports additional paper-page review. Supports projected occupation heuristics as prior art, not a valid new poecraft cut by itself.

**P4** Krishnendu Chatterjee, Tim Quatmann, Maximilian Schäffeler, Maximilian Weininger, Tobias Winkler, Daniel Zilken. *Fixed Point Certificates for Reachability and Expected Rewards in MDPs*, TACAS 2025, [paper HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.11467v1), [institutional record](https://research-explorer.ista.ac.at/record/19743). Inspected certificate separation, finite-model setup and implementation scope. Native implicit model/rounding bridge remains separate. Author spelling follows the paper/institutional record; the linked artifact metadata uses a different spelling in one field.

**P5** Ingy ElSayed-Aly, David Parker, Lu Feng. *Distributional Probabilistic Model Checking*, NFM 2024, [paper HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.05584v3). Inspected distinction between fixed-chain distribution precision and MDP distributional-iteration stopping threshold. Not a cheap exact solution for every large primitive horizon.

**P6** Anders Hansson, Bo Wahlberg. *Performance Bounds for Rollout Policies in Stochastic Shortest Path Problems* (May 2026), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22965). Abstract checked to confirm the audit's description. No theorem transfer or implementation depends on it. Uniform approximation/properness/hitting-time premises are not established for this project.

Classical algebra below is derived explicitly and checked on new finite rational models. No novelty claim is made for Schur complements, inverse updates, occupancy measures, bisimulation or weighted residual bounds. This is a targeted literature review, not exhaustive novelty certification.
