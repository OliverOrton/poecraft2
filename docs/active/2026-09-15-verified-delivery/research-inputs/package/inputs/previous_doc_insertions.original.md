### A new suspension point must preserve the logical transaction

Let W contain committed evidence K, an active task cursor and staged scratch, and
let alpha(W)=K omit unfinished scratch. A sufficient condition for safe finer
slicing is that every fine step either leaves alpha unchanged or makes a complete
logical commit permitted by the original work owner. Cancellation/finish discards

steps then preserves the complete-row and certificate invariants at every prefix.

In particular, all positive-mass outcomes, prices and permitted observation choices

logical-work debit. A moved lower snapshot or temporary action admission retains
its dedicated rollback owner.
