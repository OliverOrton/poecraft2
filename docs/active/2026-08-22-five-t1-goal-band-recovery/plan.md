# Five-T1 Goal-Band Recovery

**Status: complete and accepted locally (2026-08-22).** See the
[result](result.md).

Parent: [documentation map](../../README.md)

## Objective

Recover a useful five-T1 strategy by treating Oliver's exact four-of-five
Conquest Lamellar carrier as the primary last-mile problem, then reuse proved
continuation values for useful four-of-five carrier classes when solving from
earlier progress. Do not force a fixed goal order or collapse distinct exact
carriers merely because they satisfy the same goal-slot mask.

## Primary witness

- rare item-level-86 Conquest Lamellar;
- exact natural T1 Armour/Evasion, hybrid Armour/Evasion/Stun Recovery, and
  flat Armour/Evasion prefixes;
- fractured natural T1 Spell Suppression suffix;
- missing natural T1 additional Physical Damage Reduction suffix;
- published Allflame economy;
- voluntary economic Restart and automatic Imprint programs disabled.

Oliver's release-WASM run after 29m52s remained in reachable-state expansion:
19,055 states, 17,382 expanded, 460,358 rows, 1,059,063 transitions, 14,698,830
reforge work, 148 focused rounds, 630.3 MiB solver-owned memory, zero refinement
states, and zero certification pairs. Its upper was 13,276.82 chaos and lower
0.01165 chaos. This makes action generation/scheduling—not strict refinement
or compiled evaluation—the initial measured owner.

## Fixed boundaries

- Preserve exact mechanics, action pricing authority, and final publication
  proof requirements.
- A goal-band continuation is keyed by the full exact carrier/observation
  authority needed by future actions, not only satisfied-slot count or mask.
- Goal-band reuse must allow one action to produce multiple goals and must not
  impose a fixed crafting order.
- A destructive action is pruned only by an admissible successor-aware lower
  bound or a complete behavior-preserving dominance proof.
- Provisional policy estimates and independently verified executable uppers
  are distinct. A displayed verified upper may not increase.
- Do not solve this milestone with an arbitrary cap increase, missing-price
  fallback, heuristic action exclusion, or an exactness downgrade.
- Inspect history before implementing a candidate optimization; retain prior
  accepted infrastructure and do not repeat reverted experiments.
- Run the final changed-layer acceptance once. Do not run the full repository
  pipeline unless this boundary becomes the explicitly selected merge gate.

## Work plan

1. Reproduce the exact partial carrier natively and audit all missing prices,
   admitted primitive/fixed options, preservation contracts, reach masks,
   root Q bounds, delayed-family scheduling, and repeated reforge work.
2. Add a last-mile action census that identifies the cheapest legal programs
   capable of preserving the four goals and reaching Physical Damage
   Reduction. Confirm Harvest, Fossil, Eldritch, metamod, Fracture, and ordinary
   currency disposition from engine evidence.
3. Implement the smallest exact goal-band continuation cache supported by the
   census. Solve useful four-of-five exact carrier classes first and use their
   certified continuation values as terminal boundary values for earlier
   progress without conflating carriers or closing an open action family.
4. Materialize competitive executable incumbents before mutable frontier
   provenance expires; keep an immutable verified portfolio and expose
   provisional versus verified bounds separately.
5. Apply the continuation boundary to the from-empty five-T1 witness. Reduce
   repeated action-row/reforge work, make long evaluation stages cooperative,
   and retain the cheapest independently evaluated executable result.

## Acceptance

- The exact four-of-five primary publishes a materially useful verified
  strategy inside the five-minute product boundary, or names one precise
  remaining resource/work owner with the best candidate preserved.
- Once independently verified, its upper is monotonic through later action
  discovery and publication.
- Missing price keys and every relevant action family have explicit audited
  dispositions.
- From-empty five-T1 uses proved four-of-five continuations rather than
  degenerating to an unexplained Chaos renewal.
- Warlord, Imprint retry, four-T1, and a non-armour partial-five control retain
  their proof/result classifications.
- Focused native tests, native build, release WASM, downstream web tests,
  TypeScript, and `git diff --check` pass.
- HANDOFF records native/WASM wall time, work, memory, bounds, graph census,
  verification, and remaining limitations. Local commits carry the required
  co-author line; no push is issued.
