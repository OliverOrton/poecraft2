## Closure

Gate 1 proved a cheap admissible envelope from the mandatory first setup
primitive, but the isolated Gate 2 prototype showed that automatic-action
separation alone does not address the product failure. It deferred every
temporary-bench descriptor on all four hard cases, after which the first
ordinary broad reforge still discovered 200,000 states from the start carrier
with `L=0` and no finite `U`.

No production source or generated output was retained. The complete result,
measurements, and next design boundary are in the [final report](report.md).

## Objective

Implement the smallest exact architecture that separates complete,
lightweight state-local automatic-action descriptors from expensive local
transition-kernel construction. An unseen descriptor must retain a cheap
admissible lower envelope, and exact materialization must occur whenever an
unseen action could improve the incumbent or violate the Bellman certificate.

The desired product result is earlier finite upper policies and faster
certified-gap progress on three- and four-natural-T1 goals without weakening:

`L <= J_pi <= U`.

Restricted-action values are search guidance only. They never become the
proof-bearing lower bound while unresolved action descriptors remain.

## Scope And Invariants

- exact small synthetic/regression controls;
- the five-case portfolio pinned by the accepted research report;
- native and non-visual WASM acceptance; and
- documentation and rebuilt derived WASM output required by an engine change.

Out of scope unless this plan is explicitly revised:

- Path of Exile mechanic behavior or rulings;
- action vocabulary or action scope;
- economy prices, snapshots, ingest, or publishing;
- product defaults or public resource caps;
- public C ABI, strategy schema, compiler, or UI behavior;
- tracked natural-T1 corpus changes;
- the exact natural two-T1 oracle; and
- browser visual review.

The descriptor enumeration must be complete and deterministic. Descriptor
identity, envelope inputs, and any materialization decision must invalidate
under every price, goal, action-vocabulary, or carrier change that can affect
the exact operator.

If no useful cheap envelope can be proved, or exact separation cannot be
integrated without presenting a restricted-action value as certified, stop
with the negative result. Do not substitute heuristic-only admission.

## Gate 1 — Descriptor Envelope Proof

- Enumerate every current state-local automatic action without constructing
  its exact kernel.
- Define a cheap descriptor lower envelope entirely from already admissible
  quantities.
- Prove the envelope is no greater than the exact Bellman action term for all
  legal carriers, goals, prices, and retry/self-loop behavior.
- Add diagnostic telemetry only where current retained evidence cannot measure
  per-descriptor envelope, local discovered-state delta, reforge-work delta,
  and deterministic identity.
- Check repricing and action-vocabulary invalidation explicitly.

Exit: a written proof plus exact small controls, or a recorded rejection.
Generic zero is safe but fails the utility gate.

## Gate 2 — Isolated Separation Qualification

- Build a production-shaped isolated prototype using complete descriptors,
  deterministic envelope ordering, exact kernel materialization, and a
  violation/separation test.
- Keep the proof-bearing lower bound at the minimum of materialized exact
  Bellman terms and every unresolved admissible descriptor envelope.
- Show that deferred descriptors are eventually materialized when needed for
  the incumbent or requested certificate.
- Measure kernel, local-state, reforge-work, and memory avoidance on the pinned
  traces before integration.

Exit: exact parity on small controls and material avoidance large enough to
address the measured first-carrier failure. Otherwise stop.
