## Outcome

The opt-in goal-progress-gated solver now retains completed delayed rows,
attributes their exact Q-interval uncertainty to successor states, expands
bounded high-impact batches, refreshes executable upper policies, and
reconsiders rows without recomputing their probability distributions.
Exceptional-support states must be expanded before their action is classified.

The frozen full-four case moved beyond the former 32-state checkpoint to 8,908
expanded states and raised completed alternative lower-Q values from roughly
`3`–`89` to `433`–`520` chaos. Its upper ends remain close to the 60.3-million
Chaos incumbent, so the rows and envelope remain honestly unresolved. The
bounded deep-four diagnostic reached 11,570 expanded states, raised the root
lower bound to `432.410495`, and improved the executable upper to
`185,688,633.771637` before the diagnostic was stopped.

Eligible rare helmets, body armour, gloves, and boots now receive four
goal-relevant automatic compound options: Eldritch Annul Prefix/Suffix and
Eldritch Chaos Prefix/Suffix. They use real priced Ember/Ichor setup only when
needed, retain exact item state, and compile to the real setup and final
currency operations. Manual Eldritch behavior and the unrestricted solver are
unchanged.
