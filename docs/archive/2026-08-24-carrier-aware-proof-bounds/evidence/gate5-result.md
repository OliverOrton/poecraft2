## Retained consumers

The Gate 3 carrier lower now participates in operator proof lowers as an
independent lower on every concrete Q value. Exact destructive rows may use
the same authority in preservation comparison; it never supplies executable
policy, constructive upper, or closure authority. Economic Restart is
represented in the carrier MDP by its priced exact successor: a fresh Normal
carrier with satisfied mask zero.

Focused lower initialization publishes the independent carrier/pattern floor
for non-Eldritch as well as Eldritch sessions. While an incremental requested
action envelope remains open, the public lower remains that independent floor;
the restricted-search value is never promoted. Telemetry now reports four
separate authorities:

- independent pattern/carrier lower;
- restricted-search lower and whether its envelope is global;
- unresolved-descriptor authority, explicitly inactive after Gate 4; and
- exact-closure lower, present only for an exact policy closure.

The strict Eldritch guard remains. Legacy focused and incremental carrier
ordering is unchanged.
