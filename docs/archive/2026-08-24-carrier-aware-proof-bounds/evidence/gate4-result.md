# Gate 4 — Unresolved-Action Lower Descriptors

**Result: stopped and restored on 2026-08-24.**

Parent: [Carrier-Aware Proof Bounds And Consumers](../plan.md)

## Prototype boundary

The archived mandatory-first-price architecture was reproduced as an
observational native prototype. It retained one price-scoped lower-only record
for every known carrier/operator obligation, distinguished the initial broad
Chaos anchor, delayed Essence/Fossil/Harvest primitives, and synthesized
automatic options, and marked a record resolved only after an exact row or
exact inapplicability decision.

The prototype had no sparse-row, policy, upper, pruning, or closure authority.
For covered ordinary primitive shapes it strengthened the existing
immediate/universal action lower with the Gate 3 carrier Bellman lower. It did
not apply that continuation inference to automatic programs whose complete
successor option shape was not represented by the measurement. The source was
removed after measurement; no descriptor implementation remains in the
working tree.

## Decision

Gate 4 repeats its declared archived failure. Carrier continuations strengthen
some records but cheap alternatives still pin the lower minimum, no descriptor
strictly separates, and observational descriptors do not improve published
lower or work. Shipping the machinery would add state without a useful proof
consumer.

Only the Gate 4 source experiment was removed. Gate 3 remains valid and Gate 5
continues with the carrier-aware completion bound.
