## What Was Retained

seam that can retain one failed ladder selected-policy prefix before ordinary
restore, bind it to complete identities, classify typed goal/frontier/
unresolved stops, and replay only its selected rows from the authored exact

The seam is independently capped, cooperative, cancellation-safe, and
fail-closed. It preserves complete exact item identity and never materializes
a representative coarse state, searches alternatives, evaluates a replacement
policy, or publishes a value. Ordinary solve finalization is frozen before the
diagnostic and private work is excluded from ordinary authority and budgets.

The repository CLI can derive immutable off/record/recover revisions, bind the
complete private request in full identity while comparing equal core input,
and hash nine ordinary-result components independently. No MCP, GUI, direct
SQL, catalog editing, public ABI, strategy vocabulary, mechanic, or product
surface was added.

## What The Evidence Says

Matched cumulative-10 off/record/recover/replay runs had unequal full requests
but equal core identity, every enumerated core component, every ordinary
component, deterministic work, bounds, strategy bytes, exact evaluation, and
ordinary result identity.

The retained boundary identities are stable, but exact replay reaches
`non-goal coarse state 213 has no selected policy action` before the named
missing parent. Recovery therefore truthfully returns `invalid_prefix` with
incomplete support, no absorption proof, and zero exact entry members. A real
private prefix cap refused at one retained state; a genuinely long-running
attempt was canceled through the CLI with terminal process-tree removal and
released reservation. A same-key investigation-bundle replay returned the
same bundle and artifact identities.

This proves the boundary seam and reveals the actual prerequisite defect
shape. It does **not** establish a usable exact continuation entry, fragment
domain, rejoin disposition, or better policy.
