## Objective

Extend the existing exact-goal carrier ladder with the smallest bounded online
continuation-completion lane that can turn one ladder-selected open
continuation obligation into a mechanically verified local policy and rejoin
ordinary policy assembly.

The ordinary ladder remains the planner and fallback. It alone discovers and
selects entry carriers and target exits. A private bounded search may propose
finite local control, but the existing fragment authority firewall remains:
engine-owned mechanics rebuild every primitive transition, verification must
prove complete mass and properness, and an independently evaluated ordinary
strategy is required before any candidate may influence an executable upper.

This is not a fragment library, a second global planner, or stored item/base
advice. Continuations are solve-local artifacts bound to the exact entry,
goal, action envelope, economy, data, executable, and verifier identities.

## Locked Authority And Scope

1. The existing carrier ladder is the only source of entry candidates and
   requested goal-subset/continuation exits.
2. The private builder receives one exact captured entry and one finite exit
   contract. It may not scan a permanent item/base catalogue or synthesize a
   reusable symbolic domain.
3. Proposal estimates are ordering evidence only. They may not become
   probabilities, proof lowers, pruning facts, ledger retirement, published
   values, or executable uppers.
4. Exact fragment verification rebuilds primitive transitions from native
   mechanics, requires complete unnormalized mass, rejects closed livelock,
   and retains exact exit identities and resource vectors.
5. A verified fragment is still not an upper. The complete composed ordinary
   strategy must compile and independently exact-evaluate with success
   probability one, zero off-policy mass, and complete cost reconciliation.
6. Only after that assertion may an explicitly enabled benchmark-private lane
   submit the candidate through the existing incumbent/publication authority.
   There is no direct numeric or structural shortcut into Bellman values.
7. The ordinary solve remains available as a matched control and unchanged
   fallback. Product defaults remain off throughout v1.
8. No mechanic, action kind, strategy operation/condition vocabulary, public
   request, C ABI, Python binding, web protocol, GUI, or canonical data change
   is authorized.
9. Common native source changes require the normal release-WASM rebuild and
   parity acceptance even though the new lane remains disabled by default.
10. The preserved untracked file `0` is user state. Do not read, alter, stage,
    clean, rename, or commit it. Stop if any other unexpected dirty path
    appears.

## Required Identities And Budgets

Every continuation attempt and accepted candidate must bind at least:

- exact entry item stable key and complete hard execution state;
- entry source state/operator and ladder obligation identity;
- exact requested exit contract and goal/subset identity;
- caller action scope and disabled-family identity;
- generated-operator/action-envelope identity;
- pinned economy and complete resource-price identity;
- mechanics/data artifact and executable/source identity;
- condition, state-key, verifier, properness, and tolerance versions;
- builder policy/version and deterministic seed or ordering identity; and
- private state, transition, work, byte, and wall-time caps.

Private continuation work is separately counted. It must yield cooperatively,
honor cancellation and the named caps, and never borrow unaccounted storage or
work from the ordinary solver. Matched comparisons use equal total wall/work
and memory allowances, including continuation overhead.

## Valid Completion Outcomes

### Diagnosis-only

Passes when the exact ladder-owned obligation and composition blocker are
fully attributed, no sound bridge qualifies, every speculative behavior
change is removed, and the result identifies the next concrete owner.

### Shadow-only

Passes when a bounded continuation is proposed, exactly verified, composed,
and independently evaluated but does not beat or close more than the ordinary
ladder under matched total budgets. Product/default behavior remains
unchanged.

### Benchmark-private repair

Additionally requires a strictly useful independently evaluated ordinary
candidate, successful promotion only through existing incumbent/publication
authority, improved continuation closure or policy quality at matched total
budgets, and no regression across Gate 5 and final acceptance. Product
defaults remain off; product integration requires a later selected boundary.
