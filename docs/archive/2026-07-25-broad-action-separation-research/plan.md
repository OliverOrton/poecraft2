## Objective

Determine the smallest exact architecture that can avoid the first ordinary
broad-reforge 200,000-state failure while preserving:

`L <= J_pi <= U`.

Compare three coupled ideas rather than assuming one is sufficient:

1. complete unresolved descriptors and exact separation for ordinary broad
   stochastic actions;
2. exact finite-upper acquisition from a destructive renewal policy before
   broad successor materialization; and
3. compact or symbolic exact representation of a broad reforge kernel.

This is a bounded research/design milestone. It may use isolated native
diagnostic prototypes, but it does not authorize production integration.

## Scope And Invariants

In scope:

- existing ordinary broad reforge operators already admitted by the product
  solver;
- descriptor identity, admissible action envelopes, and certificate
  accounting;
- exact streaming or aggregated success-probability calculation for a
  destructive renewal policy;
- exact representability limits for compact broad kernels;
- the four pinned hard cases and the existing smoke control; and
- diagnostic telemetry and raw evidence needed to compare the candidates.

Out of scope:

- mechanic changes or new Path of Exile rulings;
- action vocabulary or scope changes;
- economy, ingest, compiled data, or tracked corpus changes;
- public caps, ABI, strategy schema, bindings, product behavior, or UI;
- production solver integration;
- heuristic pruning, approximate state aggregation, or uncertified policy
  values; and
- the exact natural two-T1 oracle.

An unresolved action may contribute only an admissible lower term. It may
never contribute to an executable upper policy. A compact outcome
representation qualifies only if it preserves every observation needed by
all admitted continuation actions; goal-only aggregation is valid for
evaluating a fixed renewal policy, not automatically for Bellman optimization.

detached 900-second watchdog with process-tree termination and survivor

`build/broad-action-separation-research/`.

## Gate 1 — Descriptor And Impossibility Analysis

- Identify the first broad operator and exact work owner on each hard trace.
- Prove the cheapest admissible descriptor envelope available without
  successor generation.
- Determine what generalized separation can certify while `U` is infinite.
- Construct a hidden-optimum control showing why an unresolved ordinary action
  cannot be discarded from the proof-bearing minimum.
- Quantify how many operators a finite incumbent could exclude using only
  their cheap envelopes.

Exit: a proof-backed separation role and explicit limits. Stop the separation
axis if it cannot do more than reorder an inevitable first huge kernel.

## Gate 2 — Pre-Expansion Renewal Upper

- Build an isolated diagnostic that evaluates a fixed destructive renewal
  policy without interning every failed successor.
- Prove the recurrence and required carrier-equivalence conditions.
- For each hard case, measure exact success mass, expected renewal cost,
  wall time, memory, and reforge work before ordinary expansion.
- Reject any candidate that omits failure observations needed by its own retry
  policy or reports an upper without an executable representable witness.

Exit: a finite exact `U` on the four hard cases with materially less work than
the 200,000-state baseline, or a recorded rejection.

## Gate 3 — Separation With An Incumbent

- Combine the Gate 1 envelopes with the Gate 2 incumbent in an isolated
  scheduler.
- Count operators pruned, deferred, and still requiring exact materialization.
- Materialize the most competitive survivor only when necessary and measure
  whether the first-carrier state wall is actually avoided.
- Preserve the minimum unresolved envelope in `L`; do not present a
  restricted-action value as certified.

Exit: useful certified ordering/material avoidance, or proof that a finite
renewal upper does not make cheap-envelope separation selective enough.

## Gate 4 — Compact-Kernel Feasibility And Recommendation

- Specify the observations that prevent goal/failure aggregation in general
  Bellman optimization.
- Prototype the smallest exact representation justified by those
  observations, if one exists without a new abstraction contract.
- Compare separation plus renewal, compact kernel representation, and their
  combination on proof strength, first-bound latency, work, memory, and
  implementation surface.
- Select at most one production architecture. Reject all candidates if none
  materially avoids the measured failure under exact contracts.

Exit: evidence-backed production recommendation or clean negative result.
